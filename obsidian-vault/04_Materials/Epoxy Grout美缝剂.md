## English (EN)

### Overview
Epoxy grout is the cheapest "premium upgrade" in any tiling project: Rp 15,000–25,000/㎡ of floor area ⚠️, yet it is what the owner's eye lands on every day. In Indonesia's humidity, cement grout molds within months in wet areas; epoxy is effectively permanent. Failures come from process, not product: premature application (tile bed not cured), dirty joints, or wiped-too-late residue.

### Properties
- **Slip resistance**: N/A（填缝材料）
- **Scratch resistance**: 高（环氧固化后）
- **Maintenance**: 极低，防霉防污不渗色
- **Durability (Indonesia climate)**: 高湿环境显著优于水泥填缝；卫浴湿区强制环氧 ⚠️

### Visual Effect
缝色与砖同色系则"隐缝"，撞色系则"描边"——美缝颜色选择直接影响大板的整体感呈现。

### Installation Requirements
- **必须工艺**：[[wet-method-tiling|湿铺法贴砖]] Step 7 勾缝/美缝工序（铺贴后 24-48h）
- **推荐人工**：[[indonesian-skilled-labor|印尼技工]]（可独立承担，需培训压缝手法 ⚠️）
- **施工禁忌**：砖缝未干透即施工（发白脱落）⚠️；余料擦洗超时（环氧固化后铲不净）⚠️；卫浴禁用水泥填缝替代

### Suitable Scenarios ✅
- 全部瓷砖/岩板铺贴的填缝
- 卫浴湿区（强制）
- 大板密缝效果（同色系隐缝）

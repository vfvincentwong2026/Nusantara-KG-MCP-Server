## English (EN)

### Overview
Art paint is the highest cost-performance premium wall finish: at Rp 45,000–120,000/㎡ ⚠️ it delivers effects that visually rival stone or veneer at 5–10× the cost. The material itself is forgiving; the applicator is not — the same bucket produces a luxury wall or a streaky mess depending entirely on the hand applying it.

### Properties
- **Slip resistance**: N/A（墙面材料）
- **Scratch resistance**: 中（丝绒类易留痕，砂岩类较耐）
- **Maintenance**: 低-中；局部修补需同批材料 ⚠️
- **Durability (Indonesia climate)**: 高湿下需防霉底漆；沿海盐雾环境建议罩面 ⚠️

### Visual Effect
丝绒光泽、大理石纹、金属渐变——灯光下的高级感是平涂乳胶漆完全无法比拟的。

- **推荐人工**：[[china-skilled-labor|中国技工]]（洗墙灯/重点墙面）；[[indonesian-skilled-labor|印尼技工]]（非重点面可用）

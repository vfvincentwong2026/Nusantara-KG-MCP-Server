## English (EN)

### Overview
SPC (stone plastic composite) gives the wood look without wood's weaknesses: fully waterproof, termite-proof, dimensionally stable in tropical humidity. It is the default "wood-look" floor for Indonesian ground floors, rental units, and budget cream-style projects. The trade-off is feel — harder and colder underfoot than real wood, and lower-quality prints look plasticky under raking light.

### Properties
- **Slip resistance**: 高（木纹压花面）
- **Scratch resistance**: 中-高（取决于耐磨层 0.3-0.5mm）⚠️
- **Maintenance**: 极低，可直接湿拖
- **Durability (Indonesia climate)**: 优异——防水防白蚁，一楼/沿海可用；暴晒阳台区需防褪色 ⚠️

### Visual Effect
远看类木地板；近看与侧光下质感弱于真木——premium 5mm 级（Marvel 类）纹理明显更真 ⚠️。

### Installation Requirements
- **必须工艺**：[[wood-spc-flooring-installation|木地板/SPC 铺设工艺]]（锁扣式）
- **推荐人工**：[[indonesian-skilled-labor|印尼技工]]（锁扣安装为标准化作业）
- **施工禁忌**：基层平整度 ≤3mm/2m 仍是铁律（锁扣断裂/响声根源）⚠️；周边 8mm 伸缩缝不可省；避免阳光暴晒区域通铺 ⚠️

### Suitable Scenarios ✅
- 一楼/地面层（防水防白蚁刚需）
- 出租物业与高流量区域
- 预算型奶油风/现代风

### Supplier Reference (Jakarta) ⚠️
- **Atelier SKU 锚定**：`WOOD-SPC-001` 4mm Rp 160,000/㎡；`WOOD-SPC-101` Premium 5mm Rp 262,000/㎡（Marvel）
- 渠道：本地建材市场多品牌；Marvel 等 premium 线经品牌经销 ⚠️

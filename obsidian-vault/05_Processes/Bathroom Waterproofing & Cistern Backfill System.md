# Bathroom Waterproofing & Sunken Cistern Backfill System (卫生间防水与下沉式沉箱回填系统)

> 🤖 本文件为沉箱防水核心工法，带 ⚠️ 的参数依据《住宅室内防水工程技术规范 JGJ 298》及下沉式卫生间专项标准。严禁使用建筑垃圾回填是印尼项目的铁律，违者必须返工。

---

## English (EN)

### Overview

Sunken bathroom slabs (cisterns) are common in Indonesian high-rise apartments. The 300–450mm void beneath the finished floor accommodates drainage pipes but becomes a ticking time bomb if backfilled improperly. **Water accumulates → leaks to the unit below → structural damage and lawsuits.** This protocol provides absolute protection through: (1) First waterproofing coat (2 layers) on the structural slab; (2) **Secondary drain pipe (φ50)** at the lowest point of the cistern to evacuate any trapped water; (3) **Expanded clay (LECA) backfill** — absolutely no bricks or debris; (4) Reinforced screed (steel mesh); (5) Second waterproofing coat (2 layers) over the screed; (6) **Two separate 24-hour ponding tests** after each waterproofing stage.

### Critical Process Parameters

|Parameter|Specification|Note|
|---|---|---|
|**Cistern Depth**|300–450mm (typical)|—|
|**First Waterproofing**|2 coats cementitious, up to 300mm on walls|On structural slab|
|**Secondary Drain Diameter**|φ50mm (min.)|At the lowest point of the cistern|
|**Secondary Drain Slope**|≥ 1% towards the drain|—|
|**Backfill Material**|**Expanded Clay (LECA)** 5–20mm|**Construction debris strictly forbidden**|
|**Reinforcement Mesh**|φ4mm @ 150×150mm (galvanized)|Over LECA|
|**Screed Overlay Thickness**|40–60mm (reinforced mortar)|—|
|**Second Waterproofing**|2 coats cementitious + tape at junctions|Over screed|
|**Shower Wall Waterproofing Height**|≥ 2000mm|—|
|**Ponding Test Duration**|**24 hours (minimum)** each stage|Water depth ≥ 20mm|

### Step-by-Step Workflow

**Step 1: Slab Cleaning & Pipe Installation**

- _Description_: Clean the structural slab. Install main drains and the **secondary drain pipe** at the lowest point. Slope the secondary drain ≥1% toward the main downpipe. Pressure-test all pipes.
    
- _Tools_: Pipe cutter, PVC glue, spirit level, pressure pump.
    
- _Acceptance_: Secondary drain slope ≥1%; no leaks under pressure test.
    

**Step 2: First Waterproofing Layer (Base)**

- _Description_: Apply 2 coats of [[cementitious-waterproofing|cementitious waterproofing]] over the slab and 300mm up the walls. Embed [[waterproof-tape|waterproof tape]] at pipe penetrations, wall-floor corners, and secondary drain exits.
    
- _Tools_: Roller, brush, tape, scissors.
    
- _Acceptance_: 2 coats; tape fully embedded; no pinholes.
    

**Step 3: First 24-Hour Ponding Test**

- _Description_: Plug all drains. Flood the cistern to ≥20mm depth. Mark the waterline. Inspect after 24 hours for level drop and inspect the ceiling below for leaks.
    
- _Tools_: Plugs, marker, flashlight.
    
- _Acceptance_: No water drop; zero leakage below.
    

**Step 4: Secondary Drain Protection & LECA Backfill**

- _Description_: Protect the secondary drain with geotextile wrap. Backfill with **expanded clay (LECA)** 5–20mm to ~50mm below the final screed level. LECA is lightweight (seismic-friendly) and non-capillary (prevents wicking).
    
- _Tools_: Shovel, rake, buckets.
    
- _Acceptance_: LECA uniform; no sharp objects; secondary drain visible/accessible.
    

**Step 5: Reinforcement & Screed Overlay**

- _Description_: Lay [[galvanized-wire-mesh|galvanized wire mesh]] (φ4@150×150). Pour 40–60mm reinforced cement-sand screed (1:3 with short fibers). Slope ≥1% toward the main floor drain. Water-cure for 7 days.
    
- _Tools_: Screed bar, trowel, level.
    
- _Acceptance_: Screed cured; no cracks; slope ≥1%.
    

**Step 6: Second Waterproofing Layer (Final Defense)**

- _Description_: Apply 2 coats of [[cementitious-waterproofing|cementitious waterproofing]] over the screed and up the walls — **≥2000mm in the shower area**, ≥300mm in dry areas. Embed [[waterproof-tape|waterproof tape]] at all corners and pipe penetrations.
    
- _Tools_: Roller, brush, tape.
    
- _Acceptance_: 2 coats; dry film thickness 1.5–2.0mm; tape fully embedded.
    

**Step 7: Second 24-Hour Ponding Test (Final Verification)**

- _Description_: Plug the main drain and conduct a second 24-hour ponding test. This is the **final verification before [[wet-method-tiling|tiling]]**. Failure means removing and redoing the second waterproofing layer.
    
- _Tools_: Plugs, marker, flashlight.
    
- _Acceptance_: No leakage; water level stable.
    

**Step 8: Protection Layer (Sacrificial)**

- _Description_: Apply a 10–20mm cementitious protection layer over the second waterproofing before tiling to prevent mechanical damage from tile installation.
    
- _Tools_: Trowel, screed bar.
    
- _Acceptance_: Protection layer uniform; no sharp protrusions.
    

### Quality Control (QC) Standards

|Criterion|Standard|Inspection Method|
|---|---|---|
|**Backfill Material**|100% LECA — zero debris|Visual + sample|
|**Secondary Drain Slope**|≥ 1%|Spirit level|
|**Waterproofing Coats**|2 coats per layer|Visual + cut sample (1.5–2.0mm)|
|**1st Ponding Test**|24h, no leakage|Water level + below-ceiling inspection|
|**2nd Ponding Test**|24h, no leakage|Water level + below-ceiling inspection|
|**Shower Waterproofing Height**|≥ 2000mm|Tape measure|

### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)

|Issue|Cause|Solution|
|---|---|---|
|**Leakage to Lower Floor**|Single waterproofing layer / No secondary drain / Debris backfill traps water|**Mandatory** double layers; install secondary drain; use LECA|
|**Cistern Odors**|Secondary drain not vented|Ensure vented connection to downpipe|
|**Screed Cracking (Seismic)**|No reinforcement mesh|Embed φ4@150 mesh; use fiber-reinforced mortar|
|**Mold Under Tiles**|Water trapped in backfill|Use LECA (drains freely); secondary drain at bottom|
|**Backfill Settlement**|Using heavy construction debris|**Strict ban** on debris; use uniform LECA|
|**Waterproofing Delamination**|Substrate dust / Dry application|Clean; dampen substrate before application|

## 中文 (ZH)

### 概览

下沉式卫生间沉箱（结构板下沉300–450mm）在印尼高层公寓中普遍存在。该空腔若回填不当，会成为**积水池 → 向楼下渗漏 → 结构损坏 + 巨额赔偿**。本规程通过以下措施提供绝对防护：(1) 结构板上第一道防水（2遍）；(2) 沉箱底部**二次排水管（φ50）**排出积聚水分；(3) **陶粒（LECA）回填**——严禁砖块/垃圾；(4) 配筋找平层（钢丝网）；(5) 找平层上第二道防水（2遍）；(6) 两道防水层**各进行一次24小时闭水试验**。

### 核心工艺参数

|参数|规格指标|备注|
|---|---|---|
|**沉箱深度**|300–450mm|—|
|**第一道防水**|水泥基2遍，上返墙面300mm|施作于结构板|
|**二次排水管径**|φ50mm（最小）|安装于沉箱最低点|
|**二次排水坡度**|≥ 1%|—|
|**回填材料**|**陶粒（LECA）5–20mm**|**严禁建筑垃圾**|
|**配筋网片**|φ4mm @ 150×150mm（镀锌）|—|
|**找平层厚度**|40–60mm（配筋砂浆）|—|
|**第二道防水**|水泥基2遍 + 节点附加网格布|施作于找平层|
|**淋浴区防水高度**|≥ 2000mm|—|
|**闭水试验时长**|**24小时（最小）** 每道|水深≥20mm|

### 工序流程

**Step 1: 结构板清理与管道安装**

- _描述_：清理结构板。安装主排水管及沉箱最低点的**二次排水管**。二次排水管坡度≥1%，坡向主立管。所有管道打压测试。
    
- _工具_：割管器、PVC胶、水平尺、打压泵。
    
- _验收_：二次排水坡度≥1%；打压测试无渗漏。
    

**Step 2: 第一道防水层（基层）**

- _描述_：在结构板及上返300mm墙面涂刷[[cementitious-waterproofing|水泥基防水涂料]]2遍（十字交叉法）。管道根部、墙地转角、二次排水出口处粘贴[[waterproof-tape|防水网格布]]加强。
    
- _工具_：滚筒、毛刷、网格布、剪刀。
    
- _验收_：涂刷2遍；网格布完全嵌入；无针孔。
    

**Step 3: 第一次24小时闭水试验**

- _描述_：封堵所有排水口。向沉箱内注水至≥20mm深度，标记水位线。24小时后检查水位下降及楼下顶面渗漏情况。
    
- _工具_：堵头、记号笔、手电筒。
    
- _验收_：水位无下降；楼下零渗漏。
    

**Step 4: 二次排水保护与陶粒回填**

- _描述_：用土工布包裹保护二次排水口。回填**陶粒（LECA）5–20mm**至距完成面找平层标高约50mm处。陶粒轻质（抗震）且无毛细作用（阻断水份上返）。
    
- _工具_：铁锹、耙子、桶。
    
- _验收_：陶粒均匀；无尖锐物；二次排水口可通达。
    

**Step 5: 配筋网片与找平层浇筑**

- _描述_：铺设[[galvanized-wire-mesh|镀锌钢丝网]]（φ4@150×150）。浇筑40–60mm厚配筋水泥砂浆找平层（1:3掺短纤维），坡向主地漏≥1%。洒水养护7天。
    
- _工具_：刮杠、抹子、水平尺。
    
- _验收_：找平层无裂缝；坡度≥1%。
    

**Step 6: 第二道防水层（最终防线）**

- _描述_：在找平层及墙面上涂刷第二道防水（[[cementitious-waterproofing|水泥基]]，2遍）——淋浴区**≥2000mm**，干区≥300mm。所有转角及管道根部粘贴[[waterproof-tape|网格布]]加强。
    
- _工具_：滚筒、毛刷、网格布。
    
- _验收_：涂刷2遍；干膜厚度1.5–2.0mm；网格布完全嵌入。
    

**Step 7: 第二次24小时闭水试验（最终验证）**

- _描述_：封堵主地漏，进行第二次24小时闭水试验。此为**贴砖前最终验证**——若失败须铲除重做第二道防水层。
    
- _工具_：堵头、记号笔、手电筒。
    
- _验收_：无渗漏；水位稳定。
    

**Step 8: 保护层（牺牲层）施工**

- _描述_：贴砖前，在第二道防水层上施作10–20mm水泥基保护层，防止贴砖过程中的机械损伤。
    
- _工具_：抹子、刮杠。
    
- _验收_：保护层均匀；无尖锐凸起。
    

### 验收标准 (QC)

|验收项|标准|检测方法|
|---|---|---|
|**回填材料**|100%陶粒 — 零垃圾|目视+取样|
|**二次排水坡度**|≥ 1%|水平尺|
|**防水涂料遍数**|每层2遍|目视+切片（1.5–2.0mm）|
|**第一次闭水试验**|24h无渗漏|水位+楼下顶面检查|
|**第二次闭水试验**|24h无渗漏|水位+楼下顶面检查|
|**淋浴区防水高度**|≥ 2000mm|卷尺|

### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）

|问题|原因|解决方案|
|---|---|---|
|**向楼下渗漏**|仅一道防水 / 无二次排水 / 垃圾回填蓄水|**强制**双层防水+二次排水+陶粒|
|**沉箱异味**|二次排水未设通气管|确保接透气管至主立管|
|**找平层开裂（地震）**|无配筋网片|埋设φ4@150钢丝网；掺纤维|
|**砖下霉变**|回填层积水|用陶粒（自由排水）；底部二次排水|
|**回填沉降**|使用重质建筑垃圾|**严禁**垃圾；用均匀陶粒|
|**防水层剥离**|基层有灰 / 干涂|彻底清洁；涂刷前充分润湿|

### 关联知识（人力、材料与前置硬依赖）

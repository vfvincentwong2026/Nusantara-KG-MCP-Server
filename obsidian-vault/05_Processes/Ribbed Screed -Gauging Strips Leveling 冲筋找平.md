# High-Precision Ribbed Screed / Gauging Strips (冲筋找平)
> 🤖 本文件为基础精度工法模板，带 ⚠️ 参数依据《JGJ/T 17-2020 抹灰砂浆技术规程》及实际印尼项目基层偏差统计。冲筋平整度直接决定后续饰面成败。
---
## English (EN)
### Overview
Ribbed screeding (commonly known as "Chong Jin" in Chinese construction) is not a final finish but a **substrate correction layer**. It involves creating 30–50mm wide vertical mortar "ribs" (gauging strips) at intervals of ≤ 1.2m across the wall or floor surface. These ribs are perfectly aligned to the final plane using a laser level. Once cured, the spaces between the ribs are filled and struck off using a straightedge riding on the ribs, achieving a flatness tolerance of **≤ 2mm per 2m** — impossible with traditional methods. For Indonesia's high ambient temperature (≥ 30°C), the mortar mix must include a **set-retarding admixture** to extend workability from 15 minutes to at least 45 minutes.
### Critical Process Parameters
| Parameter | Specification | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rib (Gauging Strip) Spacing** | **≤ 1.2m** (vertical) ⚠️ | Ensures straightedge rigidity without bowing |
| **Rib Cross-Section** | 30–50mm wide × 10–25mm thick ⚠️ | Thickness = maximum substrate deviation + 5mm minimum |
| **Final Plane Flatness** | **≤ 2mm / 2m** ⚠️ | Critical baseline for large slabs/microcement |
| **Final Plane Verticality** | **≤ 2mm / 2m** ⚠️ | For vertical wall applications |
| **Mortar Ratio (Cementitious)** | 1:3 (Cement: Sand) + **Retarding Admixture** | — |
| **Open Working Time** | ≥ 45 minutes (at 32°C) ⚠️ | Without retarder, sets in < 20 mins |
| **Base Substrate Deviation** | ≤ 50mm (max correction) | — |
### Step-by-Step Workflow
**Step 1: Substrate Preparation & Interface Treatment**
- *Description*: Clean loose particles, oil, and dust. Apply a high-strength **interface bonding agent** (cement-based slurry with polymer) onto the concrete/brick substrate via roller or spray. This step is non-negotiable to prevent hollowing of the ribbed layer.
- *Tools*: Roller, spray gun, wire brush.
- *Acceptance*: Substrate slightly rough; interface agent fully covers; no peeling.
**Step 2: Laser Layout & Marking of Rib Positions**
- *Description*: Project a vertical or horizontal reference plane using a 360° rotating laser level. Mark the positions of the gauging strips on the substrate — typically spacing ≤1.2m apart. Place additional strips at all internal corners and around door/window openings.
- *Tools*: 360° laser level, pencil, tape measure.
- *Acceptance*: Markings precise to ±1mm; spacing consistent.
**Step 3: Fabrication of the Gauging Strips (Rib Production)**
- *Description*: Mix the cement-sand mortar (1:3) with the calculated dosage of retarder and water. Apply the mortar along the marked lines in 30–50mm widths. Immediately use a 2m aluminum straightedge (resting on the laser-projected plane) to press and trim the rib surface to the exact final plane. The rib becomes the master reference.
- *Tools*: Trowel, 2m aluminum straightedge, rubber mallet.
- *Acceptance*: All ribs perfectly coplanar; flatness ≤ 1.5mm/2m at this stage.
**Step 4: Curing of Ribs**
- *Description*: Allow the ribs to cure for 6–12 hours (cementitious) or 3–6 hours (gypsum-based) until they achieve sufficient strength to withstand straightedge pressure without deformation.
- *Tools*: Spray water bottle (for cement mortar curing).
- *Acceptance*: Ribs hard and immovable; no cracks or shrinkage gaps along the edges.
**Step 5: Filling & Screeding Between Ribs**
- *Description*: Mix a second batch of mortar (same ratio and retarder) and fill the areas between the ribs. Using the hardened ribs as rails, run a 2m straightedge in a zigzag/sawtooth motion from bottom to top (for walls) to strike off excess mortar, pulling it flush with the rib surfaces.
- *Tools*: 2m straightedge, trowel, hawk.
- *Acceptance*: Mortar fills all voids; no hollow sounding; surface level with the ribs.
**Step 6: Surface Treatment (Sponge Floats / Scratch Coat)**
- *Description*: Depending on the subsequent finish (tile vs. microcement/paint), treat the surface:
  - **For tile adhesion**: Leave a **coarse scratch coat** (mechanical key) using a scratcher comb.
  - **For microcement/paint**: Perform a **sponge float finish** to achieve a dense, closed surface without sand streaks.
- *Tools*: Scratcher comb, sponge float.
- *Acceptance*: Texture appropriate for the next layer; no hollow areas.
**Step 7: Final Curing & Moisture Management**
- *Description*: Cure cementitious screed for at least 7 days (spray water daily to prevent shrinkage cracks). **Absolutely measure substrate moisture content (< 8%) before proceeding to large-format slabs or microcement** — this is the most common point of failure in Indonesia.
- *Tools*: Moisture meter, spray bottle.
- *Acceptance*: Surface hard; moisture content ≤ 8%.
### Quality Control (QC) Standards
| Criterion | Standard | Inspection Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rib Spacing** | ≤ 1.2m | Tape measure |
| **Overall Flatness** | ≤ 2mm gap under 2m straightedge | Feeler gauge (test between ribs and over ribs) |
| **Verticality (Walls)** | ≤ 2mm deviation per 2m | 2m plumb rule |
| **Adhesion (Hollow Test)** | No hollow sound (drummy areas) | Tapping with hammer (sound detection) |
| **Moisture Content** | < 8% (before next layer) | Electronic moisture meter |
| **Rib Hardness** | No indentation under finger pressure | Visual/touch |
### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ribs Set Too Fast (Before Filling)** | No retarder / High temperature > 32°C | **Must** use retardant admixture; mix in small batches; shade construction area |
| **Hollow/Drummy Areas** | No interface agent applied / Ribs detached from substrate | Force-use bonding agent; press ribs firmly into wet substrate |
| **Straightedge Bowing (Inaccurate)** | Rib spacing > 1.2m causing 2m rule to flex | Strictly enforce ≤1.2m spacing; use rigid aluminum/steel rule |
| **Shrinkage Cracks (Between Ribs)** | Insufficient water curing / Too rapid drying | Spray water daily for 7 days; cover with wet burlap during dry season |
| **High Moisture Content (Holding up tiles/microcement)** | Rushed schedule / Not enough drying time | **Compulsory** moisture test; extend curing to 14 days in rainy season |
### Related Knowledge (Labor, Materials & Upcoming Finishes)
- **Labor**: Requires **[[screed-specialist|Precision Screed Specialist]]** who understands rib-filling techniques. Not a general laborer task.
- **Primary Material**: [[cement-sand-mortar-1-3|1:3 Cement-Sand Mortar]] + [[set-retarding-admixture|Liquid Set Retarder]].
- **Alternative (Faster)**: [[gypsum-based-leveling-compound|Gypsum-Based Leveling Compound]] (quicker dry time, but not for wet areas/bathrooms).
- **Critical Bonding Agent**: [[cementitious-interface-agent|Polymer-Cement Interface Agent]].
- **Subsequent Dependencies (Strict Sequence)**:
  - → [[large-format-slab-installation]] (requires ≤2mm flatness)
  - → [[microcement-wall-finishing]] (requires ≤2mm flatness + ≤8% moisture)
  - → [[thin-set-method]] (thinset tile).

## 中文 (ZH)
### 概览
冲筋找平是一种高精度基底校正方法，通过在墙面或地面设置间距≤1.2m、宽度30–50mm的砂浆“筋条”（基准导轨），待其硬化后，利用刮杠骑跨于筋条之上刮平中间填充区域，达到传统工艺无法企及的**≤2mm/2m**平整度。针对印尼≥30°C高温环境，砂浆**必须添加缓凝剂**，将可操作时间从15分钟延长至至少45分钟。
### 核心工艺参数
| 参数 | 规格指标 | 备注 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **筋条（冲筋）间距** | **≤ 1.2m** ⚠️ | 保证2m刮杠刚性不挠曲 |
| **筋条截面尺寸** | 30–50mm宽 × 10–25mm厚 ⚠️ | 厚度=基层最大偏差+5mm余量 |
| **完成面平整度** | **≤ 2mm / 2m** ⚠️ | 大板/微水泥硬性门槛 |
| **完成面垂直度（墙面）** | **≤ 2mm / 2m** ⚠️ | — |
| **砂浆配比（水泥基）** | 1:3（水泥：砂）+ **缓凝剂** | — |
| **可操作时间** | ≥ 45分钟（32°C环境）⚠️ | 无缓凝剂时<20分钟即凝结 |
| **基层最大校正量** | ≤ 50mm | — |
### 工序流程
**Step 1: 基层处理与界面剂施工**
- *描述*：清理基层松散颗粒、油污和浮尘。对混凝土/砖墙基层滚涂或喷涂高强度**界面粘结剂**（聚合物水泥浆）。此步为**强制项**，用以防止冲筋层空鼓脱落。
- *工具*：滚筒、喷枪、钢丝刷。
- *验收*：基层粗糙；界面剂全覆盖；无起皮。
**Step 2: 激光放线与冲筋定位**
- *描述*：使用360°旋转激光水平仪投射垂直或水平基准面。在基层上标记冲筋位置——通常间距≤1.2m。所有阴角及门窗洞口边缘必须补设筋条。
- *工具*：360°激光水平仪、铅笔、卷尺。
- *验收*：标记精度±1mm；间距均匀一致。
**Step 3: 冲筋（制作筋条基准）**
- *描述*：将1:3水泥砂浆按计算配比加入缓凝剂和水拌合。沿标记线涂抹成30–50mm宽砂浆条。立即用2m铝合金刮杠（以激光投射面为基准）按压、修整筋条上表面至精确完成面。筋条即成为**终极基准面**。
- *工具*：抹子、2m铝合金刮杠、橡皮锤。
- *验收*：所有筋条完全共面；此阶段平整度≤1.5mm/2m。
**Step 4: 筋条养护**
- *描述*：养护6–12小时（水泥基）或3–6小时（石膏基），直至筋条具备足够强度承受刮杠压力而不变形。
- *工具*：喷水壶（水泥砂浆养护）。
- *验收*：筋条坚硬无位移；边缘无裂纹或收缩缝。
**Step 5: 刮平填充（筋条之间）**
- *描述*：拌合第二批砂浆（同配比+缓凝剂），填充筋条之间区域。以硬化筋条为导轨，用2m刮杠以锯齿形/折线形运动（墙面由下至上）刮除多余砂浆，使之与筋条表面齐平。
- *工具*：2m刮杠、抹子、托板。
- *验收*：砂浆填满所有空隙；敲击无空鼓声；表面与筋条齐平。
**Step 6: 表面处理（木抹子收面/拉毛）**
- *描述*：根据后续饰面选择处理方式：
  - **贴砖用**：用拉毛梳拉出**粗拉毛面**（机械咬合）。
  - **微水泥/涂料用**：用海绵抹刀收成**细密光面**，无砂痕。
- *工具*：拉毛梳、海绵抹刀。
- *验收*：拉毛/收面纹理符合下一层施工要求；无空鼓区域。
**Step 7: 最终养护与含水率管控**
- *描述*：水泥基找平层养护至少7天（每天喷水防收缩开裂）。**在铺贴大板或微水泥前，必须强制检测基层含水率（<8%）**——此为印尼现场最常见的失败主因。
- *工具*：含水率测试仪、喷壶。
- *验收*：表面坚硬；含水率≤8%。
### 验收标准 (QC)
| 验收项 | 标准 | 检测方法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **筋条间距** | ≤1.2m | 卷尺 |
| **整体平整度** | 2m靠尺下缝隙≤2mm | 塞尺（跨筋及筋间均测） |
| **垂直度（墙面）** | 2m内偏差≤2mm | 2m垂直靠尺 |
| **粘结力（空鼓）** | 无空鼓声 | 敲击听音法 |
| **含水率** | < 8%（下一工序前） | 电子含水率测湿仪 |
| **筋条硬度** | 指压无凹痕 | 目视/触感 |
### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）
| 问题 | 原因 | 解决方案 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **筋条凝固过快（来不及刮平）** | 未加缓凝剂 / 气温>32°C | **必须**加缓凝剂；小批量搅拌；施工区域遮阳 |
| **冲筋层空鼓** | 未用界面剂 / 筋条与基层脱离 | 强制使用界面粘结剂；将筋条用力压入湿基层 |
| **刮杠挠曲导致不平** | 筋条间距>1.2m，2m刮杠中部受力弯曲 | 严格执行≤1.2m间距；选用高刚性铝合金/钢刮杠 |
| **收缩开裂（筋间）** | 养护浇水不足 / 风干过快 | 7天内每天喷水养护；旱季覆盖湿麻袋 |
| **含水率偏高（延误贴砖/微水泥）** | 工期急迫 / 养护时间不足 | **强制**含水率测试；雨季应延长养护至14天 |

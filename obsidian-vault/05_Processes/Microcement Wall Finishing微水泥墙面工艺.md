# Microcement Wall Finishing (微水泥墙面施工工艺)
> 🤖 本文件为高端装饰工法模板，带 ⚠️ 的参数基于行业标准JG/T 157及微水泥厂家技术手册，具体项目需根据现场基面实测调整。
---
## English (EN)
### Overview
Microcement is a thin, seamless, cement-based coating that provides a minimalist industrial aesthetic. Unlike paint or wallpaper, it is a **multi-layer system** consisting of a primer, a reinforced base coat (with embedded fiberglass mesh), a fine top coat, and a protective sealant. In Indonesia's hot and humid environment, **efflorescence (salt leaching)** and **substrate cracking** are the top two killers of microcement—this protocol strictly addresses both.
### Critical Process Parameters
| Parameter | Specification | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Finished Thickness** | 2.0–3.5mm ⚠️ | Depending on substrate flatness |
| **Substrate Flatness (Pre-work)** | ≤ 1.5mm / 2m ⚠️ | Must be smoother than tile substrate |
| **Crack-Bridging Mesh** | **Mandatory** for all walls (≥ 160g/m²) ⚠️ | Anti-seismic / anti-shrinkage |
| **Ambient Temp. for Curing** | 15–30°C ⚠️ | Avoid direct sunlight and rain |
| **Relative Humidity (RH)** | **≤ 75%** during application ⚠️ | Critical for Indonesia's rainy season |
| **Sealant Coats** | Minimum 2 coats (PU or Water-based acrylic) | — |
### Step-by-Step Workflow
**Step 1: Substrate Preparation & Reinforcement**
- *Description*: Repair cracks, remove loose particles, and apply a penetrating primer to unify absorption. For concrete/plaster walls, embed the **crack-bridging fiberglass mesh** into the first base coat layer—this is a **mandatory step**, not optional, especially in seismic zones like Sumatra/Java.
- *Tools*: Putty knife, roller, scissors, mesh.
- *Acceptance*: Substrate solid, dry (moisture < 8%), and primed evenly.
**Step 2: Base Coat Application (Layer 1)**
- *Description*: Apply the first layer of microcement base material (coarser grain) with a stainless steel trowel at a thickness of ~1.5mm. Immediately embed the fiberglass mesh fully into this wet layer without wrinkling.
- *Tools*: Stainless steel trowel, hawk.
- *Acceptance*: Mesh fully covered; surface slightly rough for mechanical keying.
**Step 3: Base Coat Trimming & Drying**
- *Description*: Allow the base coat to cure for 8–12 hours (depending on humidity). In Bali/Java's humid areas, ensure adequate cross-ventilation or use dehumidifiers to prevent slow curing. Lightly sand the surface to remove trowel ridges.
- *Tools*: Sanding block (grit 80–120), dehumidifier (if needed).
- *Acceptance*: Surface flat and free of loose aggregates.
**Step 4: Top Coat Application (Layer 2 & 3)**
- *Description*: Apply the fine top coat material in two successive passes (total thickness ~1.0–1.5mm) using a flexible trowel. The second top coat should be applied immediately after the first begins to set (wet-on-wet technique) to ensure a monolithic, seamless finish.
- *Tools*: Flexible Japanese-style trowel, sponge.
- *Acceptance*: Uniform color, no visible trowel marks, no pinholes.
**Step 5: Final Sanding & Dust Removal**
- *Description*: After 24 hours, perform a delicate sanding using fine grit (120–180) to achieve a smooth, velvety touch. Vacuum and wipe the wall with a lint-free cloth to remove all dust—**critical for sealant adhesion**.
- *Tools*: Orbital sander (low speed), vacuum cleaner, lint-free cloth.
- *Acceptance*: Smooth surface, no sanding swirls, absolutely dust-free.
**Step 6: Protective Sealant Application**
- *Description*: Apply a high-performance **polyurethane (PU) or water-based acrylic sealant** in two thin coats. The first coat acts as a primer-sealer; the second coat provides stain resistance and waterproofing. In bathrooms, apply an additional anti-mold additive.
- *Tools*: Short-nap roller, brush for corners.
- *Acceptance*: Uniform gloss/sheen; water beading effect (hydrophobic); no yellowing.
**Step 7: Final Curing**
- *Description*: Allow 7 days for full chemical curing of the sealant before heavy usage. Protect the wall from splashes and mechanical scratches during this period.
- *Tools*: Protective film.
- *Acceptance*: Hard surface resistant to minor scratching; water contact angle > 90°.
### Quality Control (QC) Standards
| Criterion | Standard | Inspection Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crack Prevention** | No visible cracks under 1mm movement | Visual inspection + tape test at joints |
| **Efflorescence** | Zero white salt deposits | Visual (24h after wetting test) |
| **Flatness** | ≤ 1mm gap under 2m straightedge | Feeler gauge |
| **Sealant Uniformity** | Gloss variation < 5% across the wall | Gloss meter |
| **Adhesion Strength** | ≥ 1.0 MPa ⚠️ | Cross-cut tape test (ISO 2409) |
| **Color Consistency** | No cloudiness or patchiness | Visual under natural light (5000K) |
### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Efflorescence (White Stain)** | Substrate moisture pushing salts up / Rainy season construction | Strictly test substrate RH (<8%); apply anti-salt primer; postpone if raining |
| **Cracks (Seismic/Shrinkage)** | No mesh used / Too thick single layer | **Mandatory** embedded mesh; apply thin layers (≤1.5mm per pass) |
| **Mold/Mildew (Humidity)** | Insufficient sealant coverage / Poor ventilation | Use anti-mold PU sealant; ensure 2 coats; install exhaust fans |
| **Patchy Color (Clouding)** | Inconsistent drying speed / Direct AC or fan blow | Maintain stable environment; avoid direct airflow on wet surface |
| **Poor Sealant Adhesion** | Dust not fully removed before sealing | Vacuum + lint-free cloth wipe; perform immediately after sanding |
### Related Knowledge (Labor, Materials & Alternatives)
- **Skilled Labor**: Requires **[[microcement-specialist|Microcement Specialist]]** (training certification mandatory). Not recommended for general local tilers.
- **Primary Mesh**: [[crack-bridging-mesh|Alkali-Resistant Fiberglass Mesh (≥160g/m²)]].
- **Sealant**: [[polyurethane-sealant|Matte/Gloss PU Sealant]] or [[water-based-acrylic-sealant|Water-based Acrylic]] (for interiors).
- **Substrate Preparation**: [[wall-putty-leveling|High-strength Wall Putty]] + [[penetrating-primer|Penetrating Primer]].
- **Alternative Finish**: [[venetian-plaster|Venetian Plaster]] (more polishing work, less moisture-resistant) / [[wall-tiles|Wall Tiles]] (traditional).

## 中文 (ZH)
### 概览
微水泥是一种薄层、无缝、水泥基装饰涂层，提供极简工业美学。不同于涂料或墙纸，它是一个**多层系统**，包含底漆、增强基层（内置玻纤网）、精细面层和防护罩面剂。在印尼湿热环境下，**泛碱（盐析）** 和**基层开裂**是微水泥的头号杀手——本规程对此两项进行了严格针对。
### 核心工艺参数
| 参数 | 规格指标 | 备注 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **总完成厚度** | 2.0–3.5mm ⚠️ | 依基层平整度调整 |
| **施工前基层平整度** | ≤ 1.5mm / 2m ⚠️ | 须比铺砖基层更平 |
| **抗裂网格布** | **强制使用**（≥160g/m²）⚠️ | 抗震/抗收缩 |
| **养护环境温度** | 15–30°C ⚠️ | 避免阳光直射和雨水 |
| **施工相对湿度（RH）** | **≤ 75%** ⚠️ | 对印尼雨季至关重要 |
| **罩面剂遍数** | 最低2遍（PU或水性丙烯酸） | — |
### 工序流程
**Step 1: 基层处理与增强**
- *描述*：修补裂缝，清除松散颗粒，滚涂渗透底漆以统一吸收率。对于混凝土/抹灰墙面，**必须**将抗裂玻纤网格布压入第一遍基层料中——此为**强制项**，尤其在苏门答腊/爪哇等地震带不可省略。
- *工具*：刮刀、滚筒、剪刀、网格布。
- *验收*：基层坚实、干燥（含水率<8%）、底漆涂布均匀。
**Step 2: 基层施工（第1层）**
- *描述*：使用不锈钢抹刀涂抹第一层微水泥基层料（较粗颗粒），厚度约1.5mm。立即将玻纤网格布完全压入湿料中，确保无褶皱。
- *工具*：不锈钢抹刀、托板。
- *验收*：网格布完全覆盖；表面略粗糙以利层间咬合。
**Step 3: 基层修整与干燥**
- *描述*：基层养护8–12小时（依湿度而定）。在巴厘岛/爪哇等潮湿区域，须确保良好交叉通风或使用除湿机以防固化过慢。轻轻打磨去除抹刀纹路。
- *工具*：打磨块（80–120目）、除湿机（按需）。
- *验收*：表面平整、无松散骨料。
**Step 4: 面层施工（第2&3层）**
- *描述*：使用柔性抹刀连续批刮两遍精细面层料（总厚度~1.0–1.5mm）。第二遍应在第一遍初凝时立即进行（湿对湿工艺），以确保无缝整体效果。
- *工具*：柔性日式抹刀、海绵。
- *验收*：颜色均匀、无抹痕、无针孔。
**Step 5: 精细打磨与除尘**
- *描述*：24小时后，使用细砂纸（120–180目）精细打磨，获得光滑丝绒触感。用吸尘器和无绒布彻底清除墙面灰尘——**这对罩面剂附着力至关重要**。
- *工具*：轨道打磨机（低速）、吸尘器、无绒布。
- *验收*：表面光滑、无打磨旋纹、绝对无尘。
**Step 6: 防护罩面剂施工**
- *描述*：滚涂两遍高性能**聚氨酯（PU）或水性丙烯酸罩面剂**。第一遍作为封闭底涂，第二遍提供耐污和防水性。卫浴区域须添加防霉助剂。
- *工具*：短毛滚筒、边角毛刷。
- *验收*：光泽均匀；水珠效果（疏水）；无黄变。
**Step 7: 最终养护**
- *描述*：罩面剂完全化学固化需7天方可重度使用。期间保护墙面免受溅水和机械划伤。
- *工具*：保护膜。
- *验收*：表面坚硬，抗轻微刮擦；接触角>90°。
### 验收标准 (QC)
| 验收项 | 标准 | 检测方法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **抗裂性** | 1mm位移下无可见裂纹 | 目视 + 接缝处胶带测试 |
| **泛碱性** | 零白色盐析出物 | 目视（湿水测试24h后） |
| **平整度** | 2m靠尺下缝隙≤1mm | 塞尺 |
| **罩面均匀度** | 墙面光泽差异<5% | 光泽度仪 |
| **附着强度** | ≥1.0 MPa ⚠️ | 划格法（ISO 2409） |
| **颜色一致性** | 无云斑、无花斑 | 自然光（5000K色温）下目视 |
### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）
| 问题 | 原因 | 解决方案 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **泛碱（白霜）** | 基层水分将盐分带出 / 雨季施工 | 严格测基层RH（<8%）；涂防盐底漆；雨天停工 |
| **开裂（地震/收缩）** | 未用网格布 / 单层过厚 | **强制**铺设网格布；薄层多遍（每遍≤1.5mm） |
| **发霉（高湿）** | 罩面剂覆盖不足 / 通风差 | 使用防霉PU罩面剂；确保2遍；安装排风扇 |
| **颜色花斑** | 干燥速度不一致 / 空调或风扇直吹 | 保持环境稳定；避免气流直吹湿面 |
| **罩面剂脱层** | 打磨后除尘不彻底 | 吸尘+无绒布擦拭；打磨后立即施工 |
### 关联知识（人力、材料与替代方案）
- **技术工人**：需[[microcement-specialist|微水泥专项技师]]（强制持证）。不推荐普通本地瓦工施工。
- **主网格布**：[[crack-bridging-mesh|耐碱玻纤网格布（≥160g/m²）]]。
- **罩面剂**：[[polyurethane-sealant|哑光/亮光PU罩面剂]] 或 [[water-based-acrylic-sealant|水性丙烯酸]]（室内）。
- **基层准备**：[[wall-putty-leveling|高强度墙泥找平]] + [[penetrating-primer|渗透性底漆]]。
- **替代饰面**：[[venetian-plaster|威尼斯石膏]]（更费打磨、防潮性差）/ [[wall-tiles|墙面瓷砖]]（传统方案）。

# Premium Wall Paint / Art Paint Finish System (高端墙面涂料 / 艺术漆涂装系统)
> 🤖 本文件为高端涂装工法模板，带 ⚠️ 的参数依据《建筑涂饰工程施工及验收规程 JGJ/T 29-2015》及涂料厂家技术手册。印尼项目泛碱与霉斑是涂料的头号杀手，底漆封闭与湿度管控是生命线。
---
## English (EN)
### Overview
Premium paint finishes — whether high-gloss acrylic, matte polyurethane, or decorative art paints (Venetian plaster, metallic, textured) — demand near-perfect substrate conditions. Unlike standard paint, any substrate imperfection (pinholes, sanding marks, moisture) will be magnified. Indonesia's tropical climate introduces three specific threats: **efflorescence (salt leaching)** pushing through from the substrate, **mold growth** due to >80% ambient humidity, and **yellowing** caused by UV exposure and chemical reactions with alkaline substrates. This protocol addresses all three with a closed-layer system: (1) **anti-alkaline sealing primer**; (2) **anti-mold additive** in all wet coats; (3) **humidity-controlled application** environment.
### Critical Process Parameters
| Parameter | Specification | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Substrate Moisture Content** | **< 8%** (mandatory test) ⚠️ | Higher = efflorescence risk |
| **Substrate pH Value** | **< 10** (neutralized) ⚠️ | High alkalinity causes yellowing |
| **Substrate Flatness (Pre-paint)** | **≤ 1mm / 2m** ⚠️ | Prepared by [[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips]] + fine putty |
| **Ambient Temperature** | 15–30°C ⚠️ | — |
| **Relative Humidity (RH)** | **≤ 80%** (ideal <75%) ⚠️ | High RH causes bubbling & mold |
| **Primer Coats** | 1–2 coats (alkali-resistant, penetrating) | Mandatory for new plaster/concrete |
| **Topcoat Coats (Standard)** | Minimum 2 coats (acrylic/polyurethane) | — |
| **Art Paint (Venetian Plaster)** | 2 base coats + 2–3 finish coats (trowel-applied) | Requires specialist |
| **Recoat Interval** | 4–6 hours (at 30°C, 70% RH) ⚠️ | — |
| **Full Cure Before Use** | 7 days (for chemical curing) ⚠️ | — |
### Step-by-Step Workflow
**Step 1: Substrate Inspection, Moisture & pH Testing**
- *Description*: Inspect the wall substrate (which must have been prepared via fine-wall putty over the ribbed screed). **Measure moisture content (<8%) and pH (<10)**. If either exceeds limits, delay work. High moisture requires dehumidification; high pH requires an acid-wash neutralizer.
- *Tools*: Moisture meter, pH test paper (or surface pH meter), 2m straightedge.
- *Acceptance*: Moisture <8%; pH <10; flatness ≤1mm/2m.
**Step 2: Substrate Preparation & Dust Removal**
- *Description*: Lightly sand the entire wall surface with fine-grit sandpaper (240–320 grit) to remove any nibs, dust, or loose particles. **Vacuum and wipe with a tack cloth** — complete dust removal is critical for primer adhesion.
- *Tools*: Orbital sander, vacuum cleaner, tack cloth.
- *Acceptance*: Surface smooth; no dust residue on white glove test.
**Step 3: Anti-Alkaline Sealing Primer Application**
- *Description*: Apply 1–2 coats of a **high-penetration, alkali-resistant sealing primer** (epoxy- or acrylic-based). The primer seals the substrate's capillary pores, blocks residual alkali, and unifies absorption rates. For new concrete/plaster, 2 coats are mandatory.
- *Tools*: Roller (short-nap), brush (for corners).
- *Acceptance*: Primer fully absorbed; surface sealed; no shiny spots (indicates uneven absorption).
**Step 4: Filling Pinholes & Spot Repair**
- *Description*: After the primer has cured, inspect the surface under strong raking light. Fill any pinholes, cracks, or sanding marks with fine-surface putty. **Spot-sand** after drying and spot-prime the repaired areas.
- *Tools*: Putty knife, raking light, fine sandpaper (320 grit), small roller.
- *Acceptance*: Surface flawless; no visible defects under raking light.
**Step 5: First Topcoat Application (Base Coat)**
- *Description*: Mix the premium topcoat paint (acrylic or polyurethane) with the specified **anti-mold additive** (fungicide). Apply the first coat evenly using a **spray gun** (for flawless finish) or a **high-quality roller** (microfiber). Spraying yields a more uniform, orange-peel-free surface.
- *Tools*: HVLP spray gun (or microfiber roller), mixing paddle, viscosity cup.
- *Acceptance*: Coverage uniform; no runs, sags, or dry spots.
**Step 6: Light Sanding (Between Coats)**
- *Description*: After the first coat is dry (4–6 hours), lightly sand with **400–600 grit sandpaper** (or fine sanding sponge) to remove any dust nibs or roller stipple. **Vacuum thoroughly** before the next coat.
- *Tools*: Sanding sponge (fine), vacuum.
- *Acceptance*: Surface smooth; dust-free.
**Step 7: Second Topcoat Application (Finish Coat)**
- *Description*: Apply the final topcoat (with anti-mold additive) using the same method. For high-gloss finishes, **spray application is mandatory** — rollers leave stipple texture that is visible under gloss light. For matte finishes, rolling is acceptable but must be done in a consistent direction (avoid flashing).
- *Tools*: HVLP spray gun (gloss) / microfiber roller (matte).
- *Acceptance*: Uniform sheen; no overspray; texture consistent across the entire wall.
**Step 8: Art Paint / Venetian Plaster Application (If Applicable)**
- *Description*: For decorative art paints, this is a separate multi-step process: apply 2 base coats (trowel), then 2–3 finish coats (thin trowel layers), each burnished to achieve the desired metallic or marble-like effect. **Each layer requires 6–8 hours of drying** in Indonesian ambient conditions.
- *Tools*: Stainless steel trowel (Venetian), burnishing tool.
- *Acceptance*: Desired texture/sheen achieved; no cracking.
**Step 9: Curing & Environmental Protection**
- *Description*: Allow 7 days for full chemical curing. During this period, **maintain room temperature and avoid direct airflow (AC/fans)** — rapid drying causes micro-cracks and inconsistent sheen. Protect walls from dust and accidental contact.
- *Tools*: Protective film (floor), warning tape.
- *Acceptance*: Hard film; no imprint under fingernail pressure; color consistent.
### Quality Control (QC) Standards
| Criterion | Standard | Inspection Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Substrate Moisture** | < 8% (pre-application) | Moisture meter |
| **Substrate pH** | < 10 | Surface pH meter |
| **Surface Flatness** | ≤ 1mm gap under 2m straightedge | Feeler gauge |

| **Mold Resistance** | No mold growth after 28 days | Visual (during curing) |

| **Film Hardness** | No scratching with fingernail | Physical test |
### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Efflorescence (White salty haze)** | Substrate moisture >8% / No alkali-resistant primer | **Mandatory** moisture test; apply 2 coats of alkali-resistant primer |
| **Mold / Mildew (Black spots)** | Ambient RH >80% / No anti-mold additive | Add fungicide to all coats; use dehumidifier; maintain RH<75% during curing |
| **Yellowing (Discoloration)** | UV exposure + alkaline substrate reaction | Use UV-resistant polyurethane; apply anti-alkaline primer; add UV curtains |
| **Flashing / Uneven Sheen** | Inconsistent rolling direction / Spots sanded differently | Maintain consistent rolling direction; re-sand entire wall between coats |
| **Bubbling / Blistering** | High substrate moisture / Solvent trapped / Sunlight during drying | Ensure moisture <8%; avoid direct sunlight; apply in cooler morning hours |
| **Orange Peel (Textured surface)** | Spray pressure too low / Paint viscosity too high | Adjust spray pressure (2.0–2.5 bar); use viscometer for correct dilution |

## 中文 (ZH)
### 概览
高端涂料饰面——无论是高光丙烯酸、哑光聚氨酯还是装饰性艺术涂料（威尼斯石膏、金属漆、肌理漆）——都要求近乎完美的基层条件。与普通涂料不同，任何基层瑕疵（针孔、打磨痕、潮气）都会被放大。印尼热带气候带来三大特有威胁：基层**泛碱（盐析）**、>80%湿度导致的**霉变**，以及紫外线与碱性基层反应导致的**黄变**。本规程通过封闭层系统应对这三大问题：(1) **抗碱封闭底漆**；(2) 所有湿涂层中添加**防霉添加剂**；(3) **湿度受控的施工环境**。

### 工序流程
**Step 1: 基层检测：含水率与pH值**
- *描述*：检查墙面基层（须已由冲筋找平+细面墙泥处理完成）。**测含水率（<8%）和pH值（<10）**。任一项超标则暂停施工。含水率高需除湿；pH值高需酸洗中和处理。
- *工具*：含水率仪、pH试纸（或表面pH计）、2m靠尺。
- *验收*：含水率<8%；pH<10；平整度≤1mm/2m。
**Step 2: 基层打磨与除尘**
- *描述*：用细砂纸（240–320目）轻磨整个墙面，去除颗粒、浮灰和松散物。**吸尘后使用粘尘布擦拭**——彻底除尘是底漆附着力的关键。
- *工具*：轨道打磨机、吸尘器、粘尘布。
- *验收*：表面光滑；白手套擦拭无灰。
**Step 3: 抗碱封闭底漆施工**
- *描述*：涂刷1–2遍**高渗透抗碱封闭底漆**（环氧或丙烯酸基）。底漆封闭基层毛细孔，阻断残余碱分，统一吸收率。新混凝土/抹灰面必须做2遍。
- *工具*：滚筒（短毛）、毛刷（边角）。
- *验收*：底漆充分吸收；表面封闭；无光泽不均现象（表明吸收不均）。
**Step 4: 针孔修补与点补**
- *描述*：底漆固化后，在强侧光下检查墙面。用细面墙泥填补针孔、裂纹及打磨痕。干燥后**局部打磨**并点补底漆。
- *工具*：刮刀、强侧光灯、细砂纸（320目）、小滚筒。
- *验收*：强侧光下表面无瑕疵。
**Step 5: 第一遍面漆（中层）施工**
- *描述*：将高端面漆（丙烯酸或聚氨酯）与**防霉添加剂**（杀菌剂）按比例混合。使用**喷枪**（最完美效果）或**高品质滚筒**（超细纤维）均匀涂布第一遍。喷涂可得到更均匀、无橘皮的表面。
- *工具*：HVLP喷枪（或超细纤维滚筒）、搅拌棒、粘度杯。
- *验收*：涂布均匀；无流挂、无积漆、无漏涂。
**Step 6: 层间轻磨**
- *描述*：第一遍面漆干燥后（4–6小时），用**400–600目砂纸**（或细砂海绵）轻磨去除颗粒和滚筒纹路。**下一遍前彻底吸尘**。
- *工具*：砂海绵（细）、吸尘器。
- *验收*：表面光滑；无尘。
**Step 7: 第二遍面漆（罩面）施工**
- *描述*：以同样方法涂刷最后一道面漆（含防霉添加剂）。高光效果**必须使用喷涂**——滚筒会留下在光泽下可见的橘皮纹。哑光效果可用滚筒，但须保持方向一致（避免发花）。
- *工具*：HVLP喷枪（高光）/ 超细纤维滚筒（哑光）。
- *验收*：光泽均匀；无飞漆；全墙纹理一致。
**Step 8: 艺术涂料/威尼斯石膏施工（如适用）**
- *描述*：装饰性艺术涂料为独立多步工序：批刮2遍基层料，再批刮2–3遍面层料（薄批），每遍均需打磨出所需金属或大理石效果。**印尼环境下每层需干燥6–8小时**。
- *工具*：不锈钢抹刀（威尼斯石膏）、抛光工具。
- *验收*：达到所需纹理/光泽；无开裂。
**Step 9: 养护与环境防护**
- *描述*：完全化学固化需7天。期间**保持室温，避免直吹气流（空调/风扇）**——过快干燥会导致微裂纹和光泽不均。做好防尘和防触碰保护。
- *工具*：地面保护膜、警示带。
- *验收*：漆膜坚硬；指甲按压无痕；颜色一致。

### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）
| 问题 | 原因 | 解决方案 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **泛碱（白雾）** | 基层含水率>8% / 未用抗碱底漆 | **强制**测含水率；涂刷2遍抗碱底漆 |
| **霉斑（黑点）** | 环境RH>80% / 未加防霉剂 | 所有涂层加杀菌剂；用除湿机；养护期RH<75% |
| **黄变（色变）** | 紫外线+碱性基层反应 | 用抗UV聚氨酯；涂抗碱底漆；装遮光帘 |
| **发花/光泽不均** | 滚涂方向不一致 / 局部打磨不均 | 保持一致滚涂方向；层间整墙统一打磨 |
| **起泡/鼓包** | 基层含水率高 / 溶剂残留 / 干燥时暴晒 | 确保含水率<8%；避免阳光直射；选凉爽上午施工 |
| **橘皮（纹理粗）** | 喷涂压力过低 / 涂料粘度过高 | 调喷压至2.0–2.5bar；用粘度计正确稀释 |

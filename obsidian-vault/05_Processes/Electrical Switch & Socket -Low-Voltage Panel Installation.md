# Electrical Switch, Socket & Low-Voltage Panel Installation (开关插座 / 弱电面板安装工艺)

> 🤖 本文件为精装电气工法模板，带 ⚠️ 的参数依据《建筑电气工程施工质量验收规范 GB 50303》及印尼电气安全标准（SNI）。接线松动是电气火灾的头号隐患。

---

## English (EN)

### Overview

The installation of switches, sockets, and low-voltage panels (data, TV, telephone) is the final visible step in electrical work. Proper alignment, secure connections, and correct polarity are essential for safety and user satisfaction. Indonesia uses a **230V AC, 50Hz** electrical system; all sockets must be earthed (3-pin) and protected by an **RCD (Residual Current Device)** on each circuit. In high-humidity areas (bathrooms, kitchens, outdoor), IP44-rated or higher sockets are mandatory. This protocol covers the installation of flush-mount panels, ensuring they are perfectly aligned and flush with the finished wall surface.

### Critical Process Parameters

|Parameter|Specification|Note|
|---|---|---|
|**System Voltage**|230V AC ± 10%, 50Hz|Indonesian national standard|
|**Socket Type**|3-pin earthed (type C/F/G depending on standard)|Must include earth pin|
|**RCD Protection**|30mA trip current|Mandatory per SNI|
|**IP Rating (Bathroom/Kitchen)**|IP44 (minimum) / IP66 (outdoor)|High-humidity areas|
|**Wiring Terminals**|Screw type or push-in (spring)|**All terminals must be tightened to spec**|
|**Panel Flush Tolerance**|**≤ 0.5mm** from finished wall surface|Critical for visual quality|
|**Panel Alignment**|Within ±1mm of adjacent panels|Multi-gang alignment|
|**Wire Stripping Length**|8–10mm (screw terminals) / 10–12mm (push-in) ⚠️|Based on terminal spec|
|**Torque for Screw Terminals**|0.4–0.8 Nm (depending on wire gauge) ⚠️|Use calibrated screwdriver|

### Step-by-Step Workflow

**Step 1: Pre-Installation Site Check**

- _Description_: Verify that wall finishing (paint, wallpaper, or wood veneer) is complete. Locate all electrical boxes (flush boxes). Check that boxes are correctly positioned (height, spacing, and alignment). Remove debris from inside boxes.
    
- _Tools_: Tape measure, laser level, vacuum (for debris removal).
    
- _Acceptance_: All boxes located; wall finishing complete; boxes clean and unobstructed.
    

**Step 2: Gang Alignment Check (Multi-Switch/Socket)**

- _Description_: For multi-gang switches or sockets (2–4 gangs), check that all boxes are at the same height and perfectly aligned. **Misaligned boxes will result in visibly crooked panels**—this is a common quality complaint in Indonesian projects.
    
- _Tools_: Laser level, spirit level.
    
- _Acceptance_: All boxes coplanar; height deviation ≤1mm between adjacent boxes.
    

**Step 3: Wiring Cable Preparation**

- _Description_: Carefully pull cables from the box. Determine conductor lengths and strip the insulation (8–10mm for screw terminals, 10–12mm for push-in terminals). **Avoid nicking the copper conductor** when stripping. For stranded wire, twist the strands tightly together before termination.
    
- _Tools_: Wire stripper, cable cutter, twisting pliers.
    
- _Acceptance_: Conductor undamaged; stripped length per terminal spec; strands tightly twisted (if stranded).
    

**Step 4: Connection of Cables to Terminals**

- _Description_: Connect the cables to the switch/socket terminals:
    
    - **Live (L)**: Brown (or Red) conductor
        
    - **Neutral (N)**: Blue (or Black) conductor
        
    - **Earth (E)**: Yellow-Green conductor (must be connected)
        
- Secure each terminal screw to the specified torque. **This is the most critical electrical safety step**—loose connections cause overheating and fire. For push-in terminals, ensure full insertion (visual check through terminal window).
    
- _Tools_: Torque screwdriver (0.4–0.8 Nm), voltage tester, terminal gauge.
    
- _Acceptance_: All conductors correctly terminated; terminal screws tightened to spec; pull test (each wire withstands 10N pull).
    

**Step 5: Switch/Socket Body Installation into Box**

- _Description_: Carefully fold the conductors into the back of the box (accordion fold, not twisted) to prevent stress on terminals. Secure the switch/socket body to the box using mounting screws. Level the body before fully tightening—use a small spirit level for accuracy.
    
- _Tools_: Screwdriver, small spirit level, laser level.
    
- _Acceptance_: Body securely fixed; level within ±0.5mm; no stress on wiring.
    

**Step 6: Decorative Panel Installation**

- _Description_: Snap or screw the decorative cover plate onto the switch/socket body. Ensure it is **flush with the finished wall surface**—deeper installation leaves an unsightly shadow line, while protruding installation catches the eye negatively. Use a straightedge to check flushness.
    
- _Tools_: Straightedge, level.
    
- _Acceptance_: Panel flush (≤0.5mm from wall surface); no gaps; color matched to room décor.
    

**Step 7: Low-Voltage Panel (Data/TV/Telephone) Installation**

- _Description_: For data (RJ45), TV (coaxial or F-type), and telephone panels, terminate the cables according to the correct wiring standard. For data: use T568A or T568B (consistent throughout project). Crimp connectors securely. Mount the panel flush.
    
- _Tools_: Data cable crimper, coaxial cable crimper, cable stripper.
    
- _Acceptance_: Data cable certified (cable tester passed); panel flush.
    

**Step 8: Electrical Testing & Verification**

- _Description_: Perform the following tests:
    
    1. **Voltage test**: Verify correct voltage (220–240V) between Live and Neutral.
        
    2. **Polarity test**: Verify Live and Neutral are correctly connected (use a socket tester).
        
    3. **Earth test**: Verify earth connection is continuous and low resistance (<1Ω).
        
    4. **RCD test**: Press the test button on the RCD and verify it trips within 40ms (for 30mA RCD).
        
- _Tools_: Multimeter, socket tester, RCD tester.
    
- _Acceptance_: All tests passed; RCD trips within spec; earth resistance <1Ω.
    

**Step 9: Final Inspection & Labeling**

- _Description_: Inspect every panel for alignment, flushness, and visual defects. Label the circuit number on the inside of the switch/socket (or on the RCD panel label) for future maintenance reference.
    
- _Tools_: Label maker, fine marker.
    
- _Acceptance_: All panels visually flawless; circuits labeled.
    

### Quality Control (QC) Standards

|Criterion|Standard|Inspection Method|
|---|---|---|
|**Panel Flushness**|≤ 0.5mm from wall surface|Straightedge + feeler gauge|
|**Panel Alignment**|≤ 1mm between adjacent panels|Laser level|
|**Terminal Torque**|Per manufacturer spec (0.4–0.8 Nm)|Calibrated torque screwdriver|
|**Polarity**|Live (L) / Neutral (N) / Earth (E) correct|Socket tester|
|**Earth Resistance**|< 1Ω|Earth resistance tester|
|**RCD Trip Time**|≤ 40ms at 30mA|RCD tester|
|**Data Cable**|All 8 conductors continuity / no shorts|Data cable tester|
|**Pull Test**|Each conductor withstands 10N pull|Spring scale + individual pull|

### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)

|Issue|Cause|Solution|
|---|---|---|
|**Loose Panel / Crooked**|Box not aligned / Not leveled during installation|Use laser level to align boxes; level the body before tightening|
|**Overheating / Burning Smell**|Loose terminal connection|Torque terminals to spec; perform pull test|
|**Socket Not Working**|Loose wire / Polarity reversed / No supply|Verify with voltage tester; check polarity; test upstream circuit|
|**RCD Keeps Tripping**|Earth leakage / N-E bonding issue|Check for earth leakage; ensure neutral and earth not connected at socket|
|**Data Cable Failure**|Wrong pinout / Damaged wire|Use T568A/B consistently; check for continuity; re-terminate if needed|
|**Corrosion on Terminals (Coastal)**|High humidity / Salt air in coastal areas|Use anti-corrosion compound on terminals; use gold-plated terminals for data|

## 中文 (ZH)

### 概览

开关、插座及弱电面板（数据网络、电视、电话）的安装是电气工程的最后一道可见工序。正确的对齐、牢固的连接和正确的极性对安全和用户满意度至关重要。印尼采用**230V AC，50Hz**电气系统；所有插座必须接地（3孔）并由每回路的**RCD（剩余电流保护器）**保护。高湿区域（卫浴、厨房、户外）强制使用IP44及以上等级插座。本规程涵盖齐平面板的安装，确保其与完成墙面完美对齐和平齐。

### 核心工艺参数

|参数|规格指标|备注|
|---|---|---|
|**系统电压**|230V AC ± 10%，50Hz|印尼国家标准|
|**插座类型**|3孔接地（C/F/G型）|必须含接地极|
|**RCD保护**|30mA 动作电流|SNI强制要求|
|**IP等级（卫浴/厨房）**|IP44（最低）/ IP66（户外）|高湿区域|
|**接线端子**|螺丝型或快接式（弹簧）|**所有端子须按规格拧紧**|
|**面板齐平公差**|**距完成墙面≤0.5mm**|视觉质量关键指标|
|**面板对齐度**|相邻面板±1mm以内|多联对齐|
|**导线剥线长度**|8–10mm（螺丝端子）/ 10–12mm（快接式）⚠️|依端子规格|
|**螺丝端子扭矩**|0.4–0.8 Nm（依线径）⚠️|使用校准螺丝刀|

### 工序流程

**Step 1: 安装前现场核验**

- _描述_：确认墙面装饰（涂料、壁纸或木饰面）已完成。定位所有电气底盒。检查底盒位置正确（高度、间距、对齐）。清除盒内杂物。
    
- _工具_：卷尺、激光水平仪、吸尘器（清灰）。
    
- _验收_：所有底盒已定位；墙面装饰完成；底盒清洁无阻塞。
    

**Step 2: 多联对齐检查（多位开关/插座）**

- _描述_：对于多位开关或插座（2–4位），检查所有底盒在同一高度且完美对齐。**底盒错位会导致面板明显歪斜**——此乃印尼项目常见的质量投诉点。
    
- _工具_：激光水平仪、水平尺。
    
- _验收_：所有底盒共面；相邻底盒高度偏差≤1mm。
    

**Step 3: 导线处理**

- _描述_：从盒内小心拉出导线。确定导线长度并剥去绝缘层（螺丝端子8–10mm，快接式10–12mm）。**剥线时避免损伤铜芯**。多股线在压接前将股线紧密拧合。
    
- _工具_：剥线钳、电缆剪、拧线钳。
    
- _验收_：导体无损伤；剥线长度符合端子规格；多股线紧密拧合。
    

**Step 4: 导线端子连接**

- _描述_：将导线连接至开关/插座端子：
    
    - **火线（L）**：棕色（或红色）导线
        
    - **零线（N）**：蓝色（或黑色）导线
        
    - **地线（E）**：黄绿双色导线（**必须连接**）
        
- 将每颗端子螺丝拧紧至规定扭矩。**此为最关键的电气安全步骤**——松动连接会导致过热和火灾。快接式端子确保完全插入（通过端子窗口目视检查）。
    
- _工具_：扭力螺丝刀（0.4–0.8 Nm）、验电笔、端子规。
    
- _验收_：所有导线正确连接；端子螺丝按规格拧紧；拉力测试（每根导线承受10N拉力）。
    

**Step 5: 开关/插座主体安装**

- _描述_：将导线小心折叠入底盒后方（折叠式，非扭曲式），防止对端子造成应力。用安装螺丝将开关/插座主体固定于底盒。完全拧紧前用小型水平仪校平主体。
    
- _工具_：螺丝刀、小型水平仪、激光水平仪。
    
- _验收_：主体固定牢固；水平度±0.5mm；导线无应力。
    

**Step 6: 装饰面板安装**

- _描述_：将装饰盖板卡入或螺丝固定于开关/插座主体上。确保**与完成墙面齐平**——嵌入过深会留下不美观的阴影线，凸出安装则会视觉突兀。用直尺检查齐平度。
    
- _工具_：直尺、水平尺。
    
- _验收_：面板齐平（距墙面≤0.5mm）；无缝隙；颜色与室内装饰匹配。
    

**Step 7: 弱电面板（数据/电视/电话）安装**

- _描述_：数据（RJ45）、电视（同轴或F型）和电话面板，按正确接线标准端接。数据线：统一使用T568A或T568B标准（全项目一致）。牢固压接连接器。面板齐平安装。
    
- _工具_：数据网线钳、同轴线压接钳、剥线刀。
    
- _验收_：数据线缆认证通过（线缆测试仪通过）；面板齐平。
    

**Step 8: 电气测试与验证**

- _描述_：执行以下测试：
    
    1. **电压测试**：确认火线与零线之间电压正确（220–240V）。
        
    2. **极性测试**：确认火线和零线连接正确（使用插座测试仪）。
        
    3. **接地测试**：确认接地连接连续且低电阻（<1Ω）。
        
    4. **RCD测试**：按下RCD测试按钮，确认其在40ms内跳闸（30mA RCD）。
        
- _工具_：万用表、插座测试仪、RCD测试仪。
    
- _验收_：所有测试通过；RCD在规格内跳闸；接地电阻<1Ω。
    

**Step 9: 最终检查与标识**

- _描述_：检查每个面板的对齐度、齐平度和外观缺陷。在开关/插座内部（或RCD面板标签上）标注回路编号，以备日后维护参考。
    
- _工具_：标签机、细头记号笔。
    
- _验收_：所有面板外观无瑕疵；回路已标注。
    

### 验收标准 (QC)

|验收项|标准|检测方法|
|---|---|---|
|**面板齐平度**|距墙面≤0.5mm|直尺+塞尺|
|**面板对齐度**|相邻面板≤1mm|激光水平仪|
|**端子扭矩**|依厂家规格（0.4–0.8 Nm）|校准扭力螺丝刀|
|**极性**|火线（L）/ 零线（N）/ 地线（E）正确|插座测试仪|
|**接地电阻**|< 1Ω|接地电阻测试仪|
|**RCD跳闸时间**|30mA时≤40ms|RCD测试仪|
|**数据线缆**|8芯通断/无短路|数据线缆测试仪|
|**拉力测试**|每根导线承受10N拉力|弹簧秤+逐根拉拔|

### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）

|问题|原因|解决方案|
|---|---|---|
|**面板松动/歪斜**|底盒未对齐 / 安装时未校水平|用激光水平仪对齐底盒；拧紧前校平主体|
|**过热/烧焦味**|端子松动|按规格拧紧端子；进行拉力测试|
|**插座无电**|线头松动 / 极性接反 / 上游无电|用验电笔验证；检查极性；测试上游回路|
|**RCD频繁跳闸**|漏电 / 零地混接|检查漏电点；确保插座端零线和地线不混接|
|**数据线缆不通**|线序错误 / 线芯损伤|统一使用T568A/B标准；检查通断；必要时重做端接|
|**端子锈蚀（沿海）**|高湿度 / 沿海盐雾|端子涂防氧化膏；数据线用镀金端子|

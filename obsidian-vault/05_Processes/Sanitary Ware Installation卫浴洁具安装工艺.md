# Sanitary Ware Installation (卫浴洁具安装工艺)

> 🤖 本文件为精装卫浴核心工法，带 ⚠️ 的参数依据《建筑给水排水及采暖工程施工质量验收规范 GB 50242》及洁具厂家安装手册。防水层穿透处密封是防渗漏的关键命脉。

---

## English (EN)

### Overview

Sanitary ware installation is the final, visible step in bathroom construction—any leak or misalignment becomes a constant source of frustration and potential water damage. This protocol covers the installation of water closets (toilets), wash basins, shower systems, and faucets, with strict attention to waterproofing penetration sealing, leak-proof thread connections, precise leveling, and seismic-resistant anchoring. In Indonesia's high-humidity environment, corrosion-resistant materials (stainless steel fixings, brass with anti-corrosion coating) are mandatory. All waterproofing penetrations (floor drains, pipe exits) must be re-sealed after installation to maintain the integrity of the [[bathroom-waterproofing-cistern-system|bathroom waterproofing system]].

### Critical Process Parameters

|Parameter|Specification|Note|
|---|---|---|
|**Water Closet Mounting**|Floor-mounted (standard) / Wall-mounted (premium)|—|
|**Waste Pipe Connection**|Rigid PVC connection + wax ring / rubber gasket|Flexible connections prohibited|
|**Thread Sealant**|PTFE tape (3–5 wraps) or pipe sealant compound|Prevents water weepage|
|**Fixing Anchors (Toilet)**|Stainless steel expansion bolts (φ8–10mm)|Anti-corrosion mandatory|
|**Basin Mounting Height**|800–850mm (finished floor to basin rim) ⚠️|User comfort standard|
|**Faucet Flow Rate**|≤ 9 L/min (Indonesia water pressure: 1–3 bar) ⚠️|—|
|**Waterproofing Penetration Seal**|Anti-mold silicone + waterproof tape|After fixture installation|
|**Level Tolerance**|≤ 1mm over 1m|Laser level verification|

### Step-by-Step Workflow

**Step 1: Pre-Installation Site Check**

- _Description_: Verify that all waterproofing and tiling work is complete. Confirm that water supply lines and waste pipes are correctly positioned and pressure-tested. Check that floor drain positions align with fixture cutouts. Measure actual site dimensions against fixture specifications.
    
- _Tools_: Tape measure, laser level, pressure gauge.
    
- _Acceptance_: All pipe positions verified; waterproofing intact; tiling complete.
    

**Step 2: Water Closet (Toilet) Installation**

- _Description_: Position the water closet over the floor drain flange. Install the **wax ring or rubber gasket** over the flange, then press the toilet firmly into place. Secure with **stainless steel expansion bolts** into the floor (do not over-tighten—ceramic cracks easily). Connect the water supply hose with rubber washers. **Do not use standard steel bolts—they will rust in Indonesia's humidity.**
    
- _Tools_: Wrench, screwdriver, torque wrench (set to ≤ 5Nm for ceramic).
    
- _Acceptance_: Toilet stable; no rocking; water supply connection leak-free; wax seal fully compressed.
    

**Step 3: Water Supply Thread Sealing**

- _Description_: For all threaded connections (angle valves, hose connections, faucet tails), wrap **PTFE thread sealant tape** (3–5 wraps in the direction of threading) or apply pipe sealant compound. Over-tightening can crack fittings—use a torque wrench to avoid excessive force.
    
- _Tools_: PTFE tape, torque wrench, adjustable wrench.
    
- _Acceptance_: No water weepage; connections firm but not cracked.
    

**Step 4: Wash Basin Installation**

- _Description_: Install wall-mounted or counter-top basin. For wall-mounted basins, use **stainless steel expansion anchors** into the structural wall (not just tiles). For counter-top basins, ensure the cutout is accurately sized. Install the basin, connect the waste pipe with a P-trap, and connect the faucet supply lines.
    
- _Tools_: Drill, anchor setting tool, wrench, spirit level.
    
- _Acceptance_: Basin level (≤1mm/m); securely fixed; waste connection leak-free.
    

**Step 5: Shower System Installation**

- _Description_: Install the shower mixer valve (thermostatic or manual) into the wall rough-in. Connect hot and cold water supply lines (ensure correct orientation—left hot, right cold). Mount the shower arm, head, and hand shower bracket. Seal the wall penetration with anti-mold silicone.
    
- _Tools_: Hex key set, wrench, silicone gun.
    
- _Acceptance_: Mixer operates smoothly; no leaks at connections; shower head securely mounted.
    

**Step 6: Floor Drain & Fixture Penetration Sealing**

- _Description_: After all fixtures are installed, apply **anti-mold silicone sealant** around the base of the toilet, around the basin waste pipe, and at all fixture-to-wall/floor penetrations. This is the final defense against water intrusion into the [[bathroom-waterproofing-cistern-system|waterproofing layer]].
    
- _Tools_: Silicone gun, caulking tool, finger (with soapy water for smoothing).
    
- _Acceptance_: Silicone continuous; no gaps; water beads on surface.
    

**Step 7: Water Pressure Test & Function Check**

- _Description_: Turn on the water supply and check all connections under pressure. Flush the toilet 3–5 times. Run the basin faucet and shower for 2 minutes each. Check for leaks at every joint, under the basin, and around the toilet base. **Inspect the unit below for any signs of water staining.**
    
- _Tools_: Flashlight, tissue paper (for detecting slight weepage), moisture meter.
    
- _Acceptance_: Zero leaks; fixtures operate correctly; no water stains below.
    

**Step 8: Final Cleaning & Protection**

- _Description_: Clean all fixtures with appropriate non-abrasive cleaners. Protect the finished sanitary ware from damage during remaining construction activities (cover with protective film or cardboard).
    
- _Tools_: Soft cloth, protective film.
    
- _Acceptance_: Fixtures clean and undamaged; protected until handover.
    

### Quality Control (QC) Standards

|Criterion|Standard|Inspection Method|
|---|---|---|
|**Watertightness**|Zero leaks at all connections|Pressure test + tissue paper wipe|
|**Fixing Stability**|No movement under 20kg load|Manual force test|
|**Leveling**|≤ 1mm/m (basin, toilet rim)|Laser level / spirit level|
|**Waterproofing Penetration Seal**|100% sealed|Visual + water test|
|**Water Pressure**|1–3 bar (min. 0.5 bar for flush)|Pressure gauge|
|**Drain Flow**|Unobstructed flow; no pooling|Water pour test|

### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)

|Issue|Cause|Solution|
|---|---|---|
|**Leak at Toilet Base**|Wax ring not compressed / Toilet rocking|Ensure proper wax seal; stable mounting; use floor-mount shims if floor is uneven|
|**Water Weepage at Threads**|PTFE tape insufficient / Over or under-tightening|3–5 wraps of PTFE; use torque wrench to spec|
|**Basin Cracking**|Over-tightened wall anchors|Use rubber grommets; torque to ≤ 3Nm|
|**Rusted Fixings**|Non-stainless steel used|**Mandatory** 304 stainless steel fixings|
|**Leak Through Floor (Downstairs)**|Waterproofing penetration not re-sealed after installation|Re-seal all penetrations with anti-mold silicone + waterproof tape|
|**Low Water Pressure (Weak Flush)**|Debris in supply line / Angle valve not fully open|Flush supply lines before connection; fully open valves|

### Related Knowledge (Labor, Materials & Prerequisites)

- **Labor**: Requires **[[plumbing-specialist|Sanitary Ware / Plumbing Specialist]]** — not general tilers.
    
- **Primary Materials**: [[stainless-steel-fixings|Stainless Steel Expansion Bolts]], [[thread-sealant-tape|PTFE Thread Sealant Tape]], [[anti-mold-silicone|Anti-Mold Silicone Sealant]], [[wax-ring|Wax Ring (for toilet)]].

## 中文 (ZH)

### 概览

卫浴洁具安装是卫生间施工的最后一道可见工序——任何渗漏或安装偏差都会成为持续困扰和潜在水患的根源。本规程涵盖坐便器、洗手盆、淋浴系统和龙头的安装，严格关注防水层穿透密封、防漏螺纹连接、精确水平校准和抗震锚固。在印尼高湿环境下，耐腐蚀材料（不锈钢紧固件、防腐涂层黄铜）为强制要求。所有防水层穿透处（地漏、管道出口）在洁具安装后必须重新密封，以维护[[bathroom-waterproofing-cistern-system|卫生间防水系统]]的完整性。

### 核心工艺参数

|参数|规格指标|备注|
|---|---|---|
|**坐便器安装方式**|落地式（常规）/ 壁挂式（高端）|—|
|**排水管连接**|刚性PVC连接 + 蜡封圈/橡胶垫片|禁止柔性连接|
|**螺纹密封**|生料带（3–5圈）或管螺纹密封胶|防止慢渗|
|**固定锚栓（坐便器）**|不锈钢膨胀螺栓（φ8–10mm）|防腐强制项|
|**洗手盆安装高度**|800–850mm（完成面至盆沿）⚠️|人体工学标准|
|**龙头流量**|≤ 9 L/min（印尼水压：1–3 bar）⚠️|—|
|**防水层穿透密封**|防霉硅酮胶 + 防水网格布加强|洁具安装后进行|
|**水平公差**|1m范围内≤1mm|激光水平仪验证|

### 工序流程

**Step 1: 安装前现场核验**

- _描述_：核验所有防水和铺贴工程已完成。确认给水管线和排水管位置正确且已完成打压测试。检查地漏位置是否与洁具开孔对齐。实测现场尺寸与洁具规格对照确认。
    
- _工具_：卷尺、激光水平仪、压力表。
    
- _验收_：所有管位核验无误；防水层完好；瓷砖铺贴完成。
    

**Step 2: 坐便器安装**

- _描述_：将坐便器置于地漏法兰上方。在法兰上安装**蜡封圈或橡胶垫片**，然后将坐便器用力压入到位。用**不锈钢膨胀螺栓**固定于地面（不可过拧——陶瓷易裂）。连接给水软管并加橡胶垫片。**禁止使用普通钢制螺栓——印尼高湿环境下会快速锈蚀。**
    
- _工具_：扳手、螺丝刀、扭力扳手（陶瓷限≤5Nm）。
    
- _验收_：坐便器稳固；无晃动；给水连接无渗漏；蜡封圈完全压实。
    

**Step 3: 给水螺纹密封**

- _描述_：所有螺纹连接处（角阀、软管接头、龙头尾管），缠绕**生料带**（3–5圈，顺螺纹方向）或涂布管螺纹密封胶。过度拧紧会导致配件开裂——用扭力扳手控制力度。
    
- _工具_：生料带、扭力扳手、活动扳手。
    
- _验收_：无慢渗；连接牢固但未开裂。
    

**Step 4: 洗手盆安装**

- _描述_：安装壁挂式或台上式洗手盆。壁挂式洗手盆须使用**不锈钢膨胀锚栓**固定于结构墙体（非仅瓷砖）。台上盆须确保开孔尺寸精确。安装洗手盆，连接P型存水弯排水管，连接龙头给水管。
    
- _工具_：电钻、锚栓安装器、扳手、水平尺。
    
- _验收_：洗手盆水平（≤1mm/m）；固定牢固；排水连接无渗漏。
    

**Step 5: 淋浴系统安装**

- _描述_：将淋浴混水阀（恒温或手动）安装于墙内预埋件。连接冷热水给水管（注意方向——左热右冷）。安装淋浴臂、花洒头和手持花洒支架。墙内穿透处用防霉硅酮胶密封。
    
- _工具_：内六角扳手、扳手、胶枪。
    
- _验收_：混水阀操作顺畅；连接处无渗漏；花洒头固定牢固。
    

**Step 6: 地漏与洁具穿透处密封**

- _描述_：所有洁具安装完成后，在坐便器根部、洗手盆排水管根部及所有洁具与墙/地面穿透处涂布**防霉硅酮密封胶**。此为阻止水份侵入[[bathroom-waterproofing-cistern-system|防水层]]的最后防线。
    
- _工具_：胶枪、刮缝工具、手指（沾肥皂水抹平）。
    
- _验收_：硅酮胶连续无间断；无空隙；表面呈水珠状。
    

**Step 7: 水压测试与功能检查**

- _描述_：开启给水，带压检查所有连接点。冲水测试3–5次。开启洗手盆龙头和花洒各运行2分钟。检查每个接头、洗手盆下方和坐便器根部是否有渗漏。**检查楼下单元是否有水渍痕迹。**
    
- _工具_：手电筒、纸巾（检测慢渗）、含水率测试仪。
    
- _验收_：零渗漏；洁具功能正常；楼下无水渍。
    

**Step 8: 最终清洁与成品保护**

- _描述_：用适当非研磨清洁剂清洁所有洁具。用保护膜或纸板覆盖已安装洁具，防止后续施工损伤。
    
- _工具_：软布、保护膜。
    
- _验收_：洁具清洁完好；移交前持续保护。
    

### 验收标准 (QC)

|验收项|标准|检测方法|
|---|---|---|
|**水密性**|所有连接点零渗漏|压力测试+纸巾擦拭|
|**固定稳定性**|20kg负载下无位移|手动施力测试|
|**水平度**|≤ 1mm/m（洗手盆、坐便器沿口）|激光水平仪/水平尺|
|**防水层穿透密封**|100%密封|目视+泼水测试|
|**水压**|1–3 bar（冲水最低0.5 bar）|压力表|
|**排水通畅度**|排水顺畅无积水|倒水测试|

### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）

|问题|原因|解决方案|
|---|---|---|
|**坐便器根部渗漏**|蜡封圈未压实 / 坐便器晃动|确保蜡封圈密封到位；稳固安装；地面不平用垫片调整|
|**螺纹慢渗**|生料带不足 / 过拧或欠拧|缠绕3–5圈生料带；用扭力扳手按规格施工|
|**洗手盆开裂**|锚栓过拧|加橡胶垫片；扭矩≤3Nm|
|**紧固件锈蚀**|使用非不锈钢件|**强制**使用304不锈钢紧固件|
|**穿透处漏水（向楼下）**|洁具安装后防水层穿透处未重封|安装后所有穿透处用防霉硅酮胶+防水网格布重新密封|
|**水压不足（冲水无力）**|给水管内有杂物 / 角阀未全开|连接前冲洗给水管；角阀完全开启|

### 关联知识（人力、材料与前置硬依赖）

- **技术工人**：需[[plumbing-specialist|卫浴洁具/给排水专项技师]]——非普通瓦工。
    
- **主材**：[[stainless-steel-fixings|不锈钢膨胀螺栓]]、[[thread-sealant-tape|生料带]]、[[anti-mold-silicone|防霉硅酮密封胶]]、[[wax-ring|蜡封圈（坐便器用）]]。
    
- **强制前置**：[[bathroom-waterproofing-cistern-system|卫生间防水与沉箱回填系统]]（防水必须已完成）。
    
- **前置工序**：[[wet-method-tiling|湿铺法贴砖]] 或 [[stone-flooring-installation|天然石材安装]]（洁具安装前瓷砖必须铺贴完成）。

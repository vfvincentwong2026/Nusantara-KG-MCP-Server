# Glass Partition / Shower Enclosure Installation（玻璃隔断/淋浴房安装工艺）

> 🤖 本文件为玻璃隔断工法。印尼地震带须使用夹胶玻璃（laminated glass），禁止仅用钢化玻璃（会粉碎成颗粒导致安全事故）。

---

## English（EN）

### Overview

Tempered glass shower enclosures provide a modern, open feel while containing water within the shower zone. Systems are available in frameless (glass-to-glass clamps) or framed (aluminum/steel profiles). In seismic zones, **laminated glass is mandatory**—while tempered glass shatters into pellets upon impact, laminated glass remains intact, preventing dangerous falls through broken panels.

### Critical Process Parameters

|Parameter|Specification|Note|
|---|---|---|
|**Glass Type**|Tempered (standard) / **Laminated (seismic)**|Laminated mandatory in seismic zones|
|**Glass Thickness**|8–12mm (tempered) / 6.76–10.76mm (laminated)|—|
|**Clamp Material**|304 Stainless Steel|Anti-corrosion mandatory|
|**Water Stop**|5–8mm silicone seal at bottom|—|
|**Swing Clearance**|≥ 900mm (door swing radius)|User access|
|**Fixings**|Stainless steel anchors|Into structural wall|

### Step-by-Step Workflow

**Step 1: Site Measurement & Marking**

- _Description_: Verify floor and wall finished surfaces are level/plumb. Mark the glass partition position with a laser level. Check that the floor slope drains water away from the glass line.
    
- _Acceptance_: Marks accurate; floor slope confirmed.
    

**Step 2: Wall Anchor Installation**

- _Description_: Drill and install stainless steel anchors into structural walls (or tiles with waterproofed substrate). Use a torque wrench to set anchors to spec.
    
- _Acceptance_: Anchors torqued; no tile cracking.
    

**Step 3: Glass Panel Installation**

- _Description_: Lift glass panels with suction cups. Insert into clamps/frames and secure with set-screws. For frameless systems, use glass-to-glass clamps with rubber gaskets (prevents glass-to-metal contact).
    
- _Acceptance_: Glass plumb; clamps snug; no stress on glass edges.
    

**Step 4: Door Hardware & Hinges**

- _Description_: Install hinges and door handles. Adjust door alignment so it self-closes smoothly (if using spring hinges) or swings freely without binding.
    
- _Acceptance_: Door operates smoothly; alignment correct.
    

**Step 5: Sealing**

- _Description_: Apply anti-mold silicone along the bottom edge (between glass and floor) and side edges (glass to wall). This is the primary water barrier—any gap here will leak.
    
- _Acceptance_: Silicone continuous; no gaps; water beads on surface.
    

### Quality Control（QC）

|Criterion|Standard|Inspection Method|
|---|---|---|
|**Glass Verticality**|≤1mm/2m|Laser level|
|**Water Seal**|No water flow under/around glass|Water spray test (2 mins)|
|**Door Operation**|Smooth; no binding|Hand test|
|**Clamp Torque**|Per spec|Torque wrench|

### Common Issues & Prevention（Indonesia-Specific）

|Issue|Cause|Solution|
|---|---|---|
|**Glass Shatter (Seismic)**|Tempered glass only|Use **laminated glass** in seismic zones|
|**Water Leak**|Insufficient silicone seal|Apply continuous silicone; ensure floor slope away from glass|
|**Stuck Door**|Hinges misaligned / Clamp over-tightened|Level the glass; adjust hinges; torque clamps to spec|
|**Rusting Hardware**|Non-stainless clamps|**Mandatory** 304 stainless steel|

## 中文（ZH）

### 概览

钢化玻璃淋浴房提供现代、通透的视觉感受，同时将水限制在淋浴区域内。系统分无框（玻璃对玻璃夹持）和有框（铝/钢型材）。在地震带，**夹胶玻璃为强制要求**——钢化玻璃虽破碎成颗粒，但夹胶玻璃在破碎后仍保持整体，防止人员从破洞坠落。

### 核心工艺参数

|参数|规格指标|备注|
|---|---|---|
|**玻璃类型**|钢化（常规）/ **夹胶（地震带）**|地震带强制夹胶|
|**玻璃厚度**|8–12mm（钢化）/ 6.76–10.76mm（夹胶）|—|
|**夹持件材质**|304不锈钢|防腐强制项|
|**挡水密封**|底部5–8mm硅酮胶|—|
|**门扇开启半径**|≥900mm|使用者通行空间|
|**紧固件**|不锈钢锚栓|固定于结构墙体|

### 工序流程

**Step 1: 现场测量与划线** → 确认墙面垂直/地面水平；激光标线；确认地面坡向地漏。  
**Step 2: 墙体锚固** → 钻孔安装不锈钢锚栓（结构墙或铺贴基层）。  
**Step 3: 玻璃面板安装** → 吸盘起吊；装入夹持件/框架；用橡胶垫片隔开金属与玻璃。  
**Step 4: 门扇五金安装** → 安装合页、把手；调整门扇至顺滑开合。  
**Step 5: 打胶密封** → 底部（玻璃与地面）和侧边（玻璃与墙面）涂防霉硅酮胶——此为挡水关键。

### 验收标准（QC）

|验收项|标准|检测方法|
|---|---|---|
|**玻璃垂直度**|≤1mm/2m|激光水平仪|
|**挡水密封性**|无水流从底部/侧边渗出|泼水测试（2分钟）|
|**门扇操作**|顺畅无卡滞|手测|
|**夹持件扭矩**|依规格|扭力扳手|

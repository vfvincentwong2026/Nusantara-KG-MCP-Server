# Metal Baseboard Installation（金属踢脚线安装工艺）

> 🤖 本文件为金属踢脚线工法，适用于潮湿、白蚁高发区域。安装完成后墙面与地面交接处需做硅酮胶密封。

---

## English（EN）

### Overview

Metal baseboards offer durability, moisture resistance, and termite immunity—ideal for Indonesia's tropical climate. Available in brushed aluminum, stainless steel, or powder-coated steel with various finishes (matte, satin, or wood-grain printed). The profile includes a 10–15mm recess behind the baseboard for cable management, eliminating surface-mounted trunking.

### Critical Process Parameters

|Parameter|Specification|Note|
|---|---|---|
|**Profile Height**|60–150mm|Customizable|
|**Recess Depth**|10–15mm|For cables/conduits|
|**Material Thickness**|1.2–2.0mm ⚠️|—|
|**Fixing Type**|Stainless steel clips or adhesive|Clips for concrete; adhesive for tiles|
|**Fixing Spacing**|≤ 600mm|—|
|**Sealant**|Anti-mold silicone|At top and bottom edges|

### Step-by-Step Workflow

**Step 1: Wall & Floor Preparation**

- _Description_: Ensure wall and floor finishing (tiling, paint) is complete. Clean the junction line. Mark the baseboard height with a laser level.
    
- _Acceptance_: Surface clean; marks level.
    

**Step 2: Baseboard Cutting & Pre-fitting**

- _Description_: Cut profiles to length using a mitre saw. Pre-fit internal/external corners—mitre joints must be tight (≤0.5mm gap).
    
- _Acceptance_: Mitres accurate; gaps ≤0.5mm.
    

**Step 3: Fixing**

- _Description_: Fix baseboards using stainless steel clips (for concrete walls) or structural adhesive (for tiled walls). Maintain a 3–5mm gap above the finished floor for expansion.
    
- _Acceptance_: Firmly fixed; no movement; expansion gap maintained.
    

**Step 4: Joint Sealing**

- _Description_: Apply anti-mold silicone at the top edge (wall junction) and bottom edge (floor junction). Tool smooth for a clean finish.
    
- _Acceptance_: Silicone continuous; no gaps.
    

### Quality Control（QC）

|Criterion|Standard|Inspection Method|
|---|---|---|
|**Level**|≤1mm/2m|Laser level|
|**Mitre Gap**|≤0.5mm|Feeler gauge|
|**Fixing Security**|No movement|Hand test|
|**Silicone**|Continuous; no gaps|Visual|

### Common Issues & Prevention（Indonesia-Specific）

|Issue|Cause|Solution|
|---|---|---|
|**Corrosion / Rust**|Non-stainless material|Use aluminum or 304 stainless|
|**Mitre Gaps Opening**|Thermal expansion|Seal with silicone; allow 3–5mm floor gap|
|**Loose Fixing**|Plastic clips / Insufficient anchors|Use stainless steel clips; spacing ≤600mm|

## 中文（ZH）

### 概览

金属踢脚线兼具耐久性、防潮性和防白蚁性——非常适合印尼热带气候。可选拉丝铝、不锈钢或粉末喷涂钢，多种表面处理（哑光、缎面、木纹转印）。型材后方设有10–15mm电缆管理凹槽，无需明装线槽。

### 核心工艺参数

|参数|规格指标|备注|
|---|---|---|
|**型材高度**|60–150mm|可定制|
|**凹槽深度**|10–15mm|用于线缆/管道|
|**材料厚度**|1.2–2.0mm ⚠️|—|
|**固定方式**|不锈钢卡扣或结构胶|混凝土用卡扣；瓷砖用胶|
|**固定间距**|≤600mm|—|
|**密封胶**|防霉硅酮胶|顶部和底部|

### 工序流程

**Step 1: 墙面/地面准备** → 完成面清洁；激光打标高度线。  
**Step 2: 切割与预装** → 斜切锯切割；内外角试拼（缝隙≤0.5mm）。  
**Step 3: 固定安装** → 不锈钢卡扣或结构胶固定；离地预留3–5mm伸缩缝。  
**Step 4: 接缝密封** → 顶部（墙面）和底部（地面）打防霉硅酮胶，抹平。

### 验收标准（QC）

|验收项|标准|检测方法|
|---|---|---|
|**水平度**|≤1mm/2m|激光水平仪|
|**对角缝隙**|≤0.5mm|塞尺|
|**固定牢固度**|无松动|手测|
|**硅酮胶**|连续无间断|目视|

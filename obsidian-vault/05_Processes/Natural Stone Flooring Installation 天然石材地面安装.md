# Natural Stone Flooring Installation (天然石材地面安装)
> 🤖 本文件为天然石材专项工法，带 ⚠️ 参数依据《天然大理石建筑板材 GB/T 19766》及环氧树脂胶粘剂厂家技术手册。泛碱防治是印尼项目的生命线。
---
## English (EN)
### Overview
Natural stone (marble, travertine, andesite, granite) differs fundamentally from ceramic tiles—it is porous, anisotropic, and chemically reactive. In Indonesia's humid climate, **efflorescence (calcium/salt migration)** appears as white haze within weeks if untreated. This protocol mandates a three-layer defense: (1) **anti-efflorescence back-coating** on every stone piece before laying; (2) **flexible epoxy adhesive** (not cement mortar) for full-contact bonding; (3) **impregnating sealer** applied both pre- and post-grouting. The substrate must be a precision-ribbed screed (≤2mm/2m flatness, <8% moisture).
### Critical Process Parameters
| Parameter | Specification | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stone Moisture Content (Pre-install)** | **≤ 3%** ⚠️ | Store in ventilated area for 7 days before use |
| **Substrate Flatness** | **≤ 2mm / 2m** | Prerequisite: [[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips]] |
| **Substrate Moisture** | **< 6%** (stricter than tiles) ⚠️ | High moisture = efflorescence risk |
| **Back-Coating Coverage** | 100% coverage of all 4 sides + bottom | Prevents moisture wicking into stone pores |
| **Adhesive Type** | R2T class flexible epoxy (S2 grade) | Accommodates micro-seismic movement |
| **Epoxy Pot Life** | ≤ 30 minutes (at 30°C) ⚠️ | Small-batch mixing required |
| **Joint Width** | 2–3mm (floor) / 3–5mm (commercial) ⚠️ | Allows for thermal/seismic expansion |
| **Sealer Coats** | 2 coats (pre-grout) + 1 coat (post-grout) | — |
### Step-by-Step Workflow
**Step 1: Stone Inspection & Pre-Storage**
- *Description*: Inspect each stone slab for cracks, warpage, and color consistency. Store in a shaded, well-ventilated area for 7 days to allow internal moisture to equilibrate with ambient conditions. **Measure stone moisture content ≤3% before proceeding** — excess moisture will cause post-installation warping.
- *Tools*: Moisture meter, straightedge, color chart.
- *Acceptance*: No visible cracks; moisture ≤3%; color matching approved.
**Step 2: Anti-Efflorescence Back-Coating (Critical Defense)**
- *Description*: Apply a specialized **epoxy-based anti-efflorescence coating** to the entire back surface and all four side edges of every stone piece. This seals the capillary pores, preventing ground moisture from dissolving salts and migrating to the surface. **This step is non-negotiable** — skipping this is the #1 cause of white stains.
- *Tools*: Short-nap roller, small brush (for edges), mixing paddle.
- *Acceptance*: 100% coverage; edges fully sealed; coating dry to touch (tack-free).
**Step 3: Substrate Check & Priming**
- *Description*: Verify the ribbed screed substrate meets flatness (≤2mm/2m) and moisture (<6%). Apply a penetrating epoxy primer over the screed to bond the flexible adhesive layer and suppress residual moisture vapor.
- *Tools*: 2m straightedge, moisture meter, roller.
- *Acceptance*: Primer fully absorbed; no pooling.
**Step 4: Flexible Epoxy Adhesive Mixing & Buttering**
- *Description*: Mix the R2T flexible epoxy adhesive in small batches (pot life 30 mins). Apply a uniform coat to the substrate using a 10mm notched trowel, and **back-butter the stone** (flat trowel) to eliminate all voids. Double-buttering is mandatory for full-contact adhesion.
- *Tools*: 10mm notched trowel, flat trowel, low-speed mixer.
- *Acceptance*: Both surfaces fully coated; ribs continuous; no dry spots.
**Step 5: Stone Placement, Compaction & Leveling**
- *Description*: Place the stone onto the adhesive bed with a slight side-slip. Compact using a **vibration compactor** (or rubber mallet with a plywood pad) to expel air and achieve full bedding. Immediately insert heavy-duty leveling wedges to ensure lippage ≤0.3mm.
- *Tools*: Vacuum suction cup, vibration compactor, heavy-duty wedges, laser level.
- *Acceptance*: Flatness ≤1mm/2m; lippage ≤0.3mm; no hollow sound.
**Step 6: Curing & Protection**
- *Description*: Allow **72 hours** of curing time (standard for epoxy adhesive at 25-30°C). No foot traffic, no water splashes. Cover with protective plywood if subsequent trades are active.
- *Tools*: Protective boards, warning tape.
- *Acceptance*: Adhesive fully cured; stone immovable.
**Step 7: Grouting with Flexible Epoxy Grout**
- *Description*: Fill joints with **flexible epoxy grout** (not cementitious) to match the stone color. Cement grout is strictly prohibited—it is porous and will cause efflorescence at the joints.
- *Tools*: Grout float, shaping tool, sponge (for cleaning).
- *Acceptance*: Joints fully filled; smooth finish; no haze on stone surface.
**Step 8: Impregnating Sealer Application (Final Protection)**
- *Description*: Apply a high-quality **penetrating impregnating sealer** (solvent-based or water-based fluoro-polymer) in 2 coats before grouting and 1 coat after grouting. This creates a hydrophobic barrier that repels stains and moisture—essential for Indonesian kitchens and humid bathrooms.
- *Tools*: Roller, lamb's wool applicator.
- *Acceptance*: Water beading test (contact angle > 90°); no surface film.
### Quality Control (QC) Standards
| Criterion | Standard | Inspection Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Efflorescence** | Zero white deposits at 7 days / 28 days | Visual + wet-towel test (24h) |
| **Hollow Spots** | **0%** (full contact required) | Full tapping test |
| **Flatness** | ≤ 1mm gap under 2m straightedge | Feeler gauge |
| **Lippage** | ≤ 0.3mm between adjacent stones | Fingertip + dial gauge |
| **Sealer Performance** | Water beads; no absorption in 5 mins | Water droplet test |
| **Adhesive Coverage** | ≥ 95% (pull-off test) | Random pull-off ≥ 1.5 MPa |
### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Efflorescence (White Haze)** | No back-coating / Cement-based adhesive used / Substrate too wet | **Mandatory** back-coating; use epoxy adhesive; test substrate <6% moisture |
| **Stone Cracking (Seismic)** | Rigid cement adhesive / No expansion joints | Use R2T flexible epoxy; leave seismic joints at columns/walls |
| **Yellowing / Discoloration** | Moisture trapped under stone / Wrong sealer type | Use breathable impregnating sealer; ensure full curing before sealing |
| **Lippage (Uneven Edges)** | Insufficient vibration / No leveling wedges | Mandate vibration compactor + heavy-duty wedges |
| **Grout Haze / Stain** | Cementitious grout used / Excess grout not removed promptly | Use epoxy grout; clean with specialized cleaner within 15 mins |
### Related Knowledge (Labor, Materials & Prerequisites)
- **Labor**: Requires **[[stone-specialist|Natural Stone Installation Specialist]]** — not standard tilers. Must understand efflorescence chemistry.
- **Key Materials**: [[anti-efflorescence-back-coating|Epoxy Anti-Efflorescence Coating]], [[flexible-epoxy-adhesive|R2T Flexible Epoxy Adhesive]], [[impregnating-sealer|Fluoro-polymer Impregnating Sealer]], [[flexible-epoxy-grout|Epoxy Grout (Color-Matched)]].
- **Mandatory Prerequisite**: [[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips]] (≤2mm flatness, <6% moisture).
- **Alternative (Less Premium)**: [[cementitious-thin-set-for-stone|Polymer-Modified Cement Adhesive]] — only for interiors with low humidity, not recommended for Bali/Java coastal areas.

## 中文 (ZH)
### 概览
天然石材（大理石、石灰华、安山岩、花岗岩）与陶瓷砖有本质区别——它多孔、各向异性且化学性质活泼。在印尼潮湿气候下，若不经处理，**泛碱（钙/盐析出）** 会在数周内表现为白色雾状斑块。本规程构建三层防线：(1) 铺贴前每块石材**强制涂刷防泛碱背涂**；(2) **柔性环氧胶粘剂**（非水泥基）满粘；(3) 填缝前后双重**渗透性防护剂**密封。基层必须为精密冲筋找平层（≤2mm/2m平整度，<6%含水率）。
### 核心工艺参数
| 参数 | 规格指标 | 备注 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **石材含水率（铺贴前）** | **≤ 3%** ⚠️ | 到场后通风放置7天平衡 |
| **基层平整度** | **≤ 2mm / 2m** | 前置工序：[[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips\|冲筋找平]] |
| **基层含水率** | **< 6%**（严于瓷砖）⚠️ | 含水率=泛碱风险指标 |
| **背涂覆盖率** | 底面+四边100%覆盖 | 阻断毛细孔吸水上返 |
| **胶粘剂类型** | R2T级柔性环氧（S2级） | 吸收微地震位移 |
| **环氧可操作时间** | ≤ 30分钟（30°C环境）⚠️ | 必须小批量拌合 |
| **留缝宽度** | 2–3mm（地面）/ 3–5mm（商业）⚠️ | 预留热胀/地震伸缩 |
| **防护剂遍数** | 2遍（填缝前）+ 1遍（填缝后） | — |
### 工序流程
**Step 1: 石材检验与预存放**
- *描述*：逐块检验石板是否有裂纹、翘曲和色差。在遮阳通风处存放7天，使内部含水率与环境平衡。**铺贴前必须测石材含水率≤3%**——含水率过高会导致铺贴后翘曲变形。
- *工具*：含水率测试仪、靠尺、比色卡。
- *验收*：无可见裂纹；含水率≤3%；色差在允许范围内。
**Step 2: 防泛碱背涂（核心防线）**
- *描述*：对每块石材的底面及四个侧边涂抹专用**环氧基防泛碱背涂剂**。此举封闭毛细孔，阻止地坪水分溶解盐分并上泛至石材表面。**此步为强制项，不可商议**——省略此步是泛碱白华的头号成因。
- *工具*：短毛滚筒、小毛刷（边角）、搅拌棒。
- *验收*：100%全覆盖；边缘完全密封；涂层表干（不粘手）。
**Step 3: 基层验收与底涂**
- *描述*：核验冲筋找平基层平整度（≤2mm/2m）及含水率（<6%）。滚涂渗透性环氧底涂，以粘结柔性胶粘剂层并抑制残余水汽。
- *工具*：2m靠尺、含水率仪、滚筒。
- *验收*：底涂充分吸收；无积液。
**Step 4: 柔性环氧胶粘剂搅拌与刮涂**
- *描述*：小批量搅拌R2T柔性环氧胶粘剂（可操作时间30分钟）。用10mm齿形刮板在基层刮涂均匀胶层，并在石材背面**平刀满刮背胶**——双面刮胶是满粘的强制要求。
- *工具*：10mm齿形刮板、平抹刀、低速搅拌机。
- *验收*：双面均满涂；胶条连续；无干点。
**Step 5: 石材就位、压实与调平**
- *描述*：将石材侧滑就位于胶层上。使用**振动压实器**（或橡皮锤+胶合板垫块）由中心向外压实排气。立即插入重型调平楔子，确保相邻石材高低差≤0.3mm。
- *工具*：真空吸盘、振动压实器、重型楔子、激光水平仪。
- *验收*：平整度≤1mm/2m；高低差≤0.3mm；敲击无空鼓声。
**Step 6: 养护与保护**
- *描述*：养护**72小时**（环氧胶粘剂在25-30°C下标准固化时间）。期间严禁上人、严禁溅水。若有后续工种交叉施工，需覆盖防护胶合板。
- *工具*：防护板、警示带。
- *验收*：胶粘剂完全固化；石材无位移。
**Step 7: 柔性环氧填缝**
- *描述*：使用**柔性环氧填缝剂**（非水泥基）填充接缝，颜色与石材协调。**严禁使用水泥勾缝剂**——其多孔性会导致接缝处泛碱。
- *工具*：填缝刮板、压缝球、海绵（清洁用）。
- *验收*：接缝饱满；表面平滑；石材表面无残留雾膜。
**Step 8: 渗透性防护剂施工（最终保护）**
- *描述*：使用高品质**渗透性防护剂**（溶剂型或水性氟聚合物）在填缝前涂刷2遍，填缝后涂刷1遍。形成疏水屏障，有效抵御污渍和水分——对印尼厨房和高湿卫浴至关重要。
- *工具*：滚筒、羊毛涂抹器。
- *验收*：水珠测试（接触角>90°）；表面无成膜。
### 验收标准 (QC)
| 验收项 | 标准 | 检测方法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **泛碱性** | 7天/28天零白色析出物 | 目视 + 湿毛巾贴敷测试（24h） |
| **空鼓** | **0%**（必须满粘） | 全面敲击法 |
| **平整度** | 2m靠尺下缝隙≤1mm | 塞尺 |
| **高低差** | 相邻石材≤0.3mm | 指尖触感 + 百分表 |
| **防护性能** | 水珠状；5分钟不吸收 | 水滴测试 |
| **胶粘剂覆盖率** | ≥ 95%（拉拔测试） | 随机拉拔≥1.5 MPa |
### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）
| 问题 | 原因 | 解决方案 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **泛碱（白霜）** | 未做背涂 / 使用水泥基胶粘剂 / 基层过湿 | **强制**背涂；用环氧胶；测基层<6%含水率 |
| **地震开裂** | 刚性水泥胶 / 未设伸缩缝 | 使用R2T柔性环氧；柱边/墙边设置震缝 |
| **黄变/变色** | 石材底部水汽集聚 / 防护剂选型错误 | 使用透气型渗透防护剂；确保养护完全再密封 |
| **高低差（错台）** | 振动不足 / 未用调平楔子 | 强制振动压实+重型楔子 |
| **填缝雾膜/污染** | 使用水泥填缝 / 多余填缝剂未及时清理 | 使用环氧填缝剂；15分钟内专用清洗剂清洁 |
### 关联知识（人力、材料与前置硬依赖）
- **技术工人**：需[[stone-specialist|天然石材专项技师]]——非普通瓦工，须理解泛碱化学原理。
- **关键材料**：[[anti-efflorescence-back-coating|环氧防泛碱背涂剂]]、[[flexible-epoxy-adhesive|R2T柔性环氧胶粘剂]]、[[impregnating-sealer|氟聚合物渗透防护剂]]、[[flexible-epoxy-grout|环氧填缝剂（调色）]]。
- **强制前置**：[[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips|冲筋找平]]（≤2mm平整度，<6%含水率）。
- **降级替代（不推荐）**：[[cementitious-thin-set-for-stone|聚合物改性水泥基胶粘剂]]——仅适用于干燥室内，不推荐巴厘岛/爪哇沿海。

# Wood Veneer Panel Wall Installation (木饰面挂板安装工艺)
> 🤖 本文件为高端木作工法模板，带 ⚠️ 的参数依据《GB/T 15104-2006 装饰单板贴面人造板》及印尼SNI 01-5007-2017木材防虫处理标准。
---
## English (EN)
### Overview
Wood veneer panels deliver the warmth and natural beauty of solid wood at a fraction of the cost and weight. However, wood is a **hygroscopic material** — it expands and contracts with changes in humidity. In Indonesia's tropical climate with humidity swings from 60% to 90%, **expansion joints are not optional** — they are structural necessities. Additionally, **termites are endemic** across the archipelago; all veneer panels must be from certified kiln-dried stock and treated with anti-termite impregnation.
### Critical Process Parameters
| Parameter | Specification | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Veneer Panel Thickness** | 3–6mm (backed with plywood/MDF) ⚠️ | — |
| **Substrate Flatness** | ≤ 2mm / 2m ⚠️ | For direct-bond method |
| **Expansion Joint Width** | **3–5mm** (at all panel junctions) ⚠️ | Mandatory for humidity movement |
| **Expansion Joint Spacing** | ≤ 2.4m (horizontal) / ≤ 1.2m (vertical) ⚠️ | — |
| **Moisture Content of Veneer** | **8–12%** (at installation) ⚠️ | Must match ambient equilibrium |
| **Fixing Type** | Stainless steel (grade 304 minimum) | Anti-corrosion for coastal areas |
| **Termite Treatment** | Certified kiln-dried + impregnated | Mandatory for Indonesia |
### Step-by-Step Workflow
**Step 1: Material Acclimatization**
- *Description*: Deliver veneer panels to the project site at least **7 days before installation**. Stack panels flat with spacers to allow air circulation. Allow the panels to acclimatize to the ambient temperature and humidity — this minimizes post-installation movement.
- *Tools*: Spacers, hygrometer.
- *Acceptance*: Panel moisture content measured and recorded; within 8–12% range.
**Step 2: Substrate Preparation & Moisture Barrier**
- *Description*: Ensure the wall substrate is flat, clean, and dry. Install a **moisture barrier foil** (polyethylene or foil-faced insulation) over the entire substrate — this prevents moisture from the wall from migrating into the veneer panels. For direct-bond method, the substrate must achieve ≤2mm/2m flatness [prior ribbed screeding].
- *Tools*: Staple gun, utility knife, tape measure.
- *Acceptance*: Moisture barrier continuous, lapped at seams ≥100mm; no punctures.
**Step 3: Suspension Framework Installation (If Used)**
- *Description*: For larger panels or where substrate is uneven, install a galvanized steel or aluminum suspension framework (25×25mm or 38×38mm) using stainless steel fixings. The framework allows for leveling adjustment and creates a cavity for services.
- *Tools*: Laser level, impact driver, spirit level.
- *Acceptance*: Framework plumb and level; fixing间距 ≤ 600mm.
**Step 4: Panel Cutting & Edge Sealing**
- *Description*: Cut panels to size using a panel saw with a fine-tooth blade (to prevent veneer chipping). **Immediately seal all cut edges** with edge sealer (wax or clear lacquer) — unsealed edges are the primary entry point for moisture and termites.
- *Tools*: Panel saw, edge sealer, brush.
- *Acceptance*: Cuts clean; all edges sealed within 30 minutes of cutting.
**Step 5: Panel Fixing (Direct-Bond or Suspended)**
- *Description*:
  - **Direct-Bond**: Apply construction adhesive (polyurethane-based) to the panel back in a serpentine pattern, then press firmly onto the substrate. Use temporary bracing to hold panels in place until adhesive cures.
  - **Suspended**: Fix panels to the framework using stainless steel screws or clips. **Leave 3–5mm expansion gaps** at all panel junctions.
- *Tools*: Adhesive gun, screw gun, clamps/bracing, 3–5mm spacer shims.
- *Acceptance*: Panels firmly fixed; expansion gaps consistent; no panel-to-panel contact.
**Step 6: Filling Expansion Gaps**
- *Description*: Fill expansion gaps with **color-matched flexible filler** (acrylic or silicone-based) that can accommodate ±1mm movement. Do not use rigid fillers (epoxy/polyester) — they will crack as the wood moves.
- *Tools*: Filler gun, shaping tool, damp sponge.
- *Acceptance*: Gaps filled flush; filler flexible and color-matched.
**Step 7: Surface Finishing (If Required)**
- *Description*: Depending on the veneer type, apply a protective topcoat (matte/satin lacquer, OSMO oil, or UV-cured finish). In Indonesia's high-humidity areas, **a minimum of 2 topcoats** is recommended to seal the surface against moisture ingress.
- *Tools*: Spray gun or roller, fine sandpaper (320 grit between coats).
- *Acceptance*: Finish uniform; no runs or sags; water beads on surface.
### Quality Control (QC) Standards
| Criterion | Standard | Inspection Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Panel Moisture Content** | 8–12% at installation | Moisture meter |
| **Expansion Joints** | 3–5mm at all junctions | Feeler gauge |
| **Substrate Flatness** | ≤ 2mm/2m | 2m straightedge |
| **Fixing Security** | No movement when pushed | Hand pressure test |
| **Edge Sealing** | 100% of cut edges sealed | Visual |
| **Surface Finish** | No runs, sags, or orange peel | Visual + gloss meter |
### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Panel Warping / Cupping** | Uneven moisture absorption / No acclimatization | Acclimatize 7 days; ensure balanced humidity on both faces |
| **Expansion Joint Closure (Bucking)** | Insufficient gap / High humidity swelling | Enforce 3–5mm gaps; use flexible fillers |
| **Termite Damage** | Untreated veneer / Ground contact | **Mandatory** kiln-dried + impregnated stock; maintain 50mm clearance from floor |
| **Edge Delamination** | Unsealed edges / Moisture ingress | Seal all cut edges immediately; apply 2 topcoats |
| **Fixing Corrosion (Rust Bleed)** | Non-stainless fixings in coastal areas | Use stainless steel grade 304 minimum |

## 中文 (ZH)
### 概览
木饰面板以较低的成本和重量，呈现实木的温暖与自然美感。然而，木材是**吸湿性材料**——随湿度变化而胀缩。在印尼湿度从60%到90%波动的热带气候下，**伸缩缝不是可选项，而是结构性必要项**。此外，**白蚁在群岛各地普遍存在**；所有饰面板必须来自认证窑干库存并经过防白蚁浸渍处理。
### 核心工艺参数
| 参数 | 规格指标 | 备注 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **饰面板厚度** | 3–6mm（背衬胶合板/MDF）⚠️ | — |
| **基层平整度** | ≤ 2mm / 2m ⚠️ | 直接粘贴法适用 |
| **伸缩缝宽度** | **3–5mm**（所有板缝处）⚠️ | 湿度变形强制项 |
| **伸缩缝间距** | 水平≤2.4m / 垂直≤1.2m ⚠️ | — |
| **饰面板含水率** | **8–12%**（安装时）⚠️ | 须与环境平衡含水率匹配 |
| **紧固件类型** | 不锈钢（最低304级） | 沿海地区防腐 |
| **白蚁处理** | 认证窑干 + 浸渍处理 | 印尼强制项 |
### 工序流程
**Step 1: 材料现场平衡**
- *描述*：饰面板至少**提前7天**运抵项目现场。将板材平放堆叠，用垫块隔开以利空气流通。让板材适应当地温湿度——以最大限度减少安装后的变形。
- *工具*：垫块、湿度计。
- *验收*：板材含水率已测量并记录；在8–12%范围内。
**Step 2: 基层处理与防潮隔离层**
- *描述*：确保墙面基层平整、干净、干燥。在整个基层上安装**防潮隔离膜**（聚乙烯或覆铝箔保温板）——防止墙体水分迁移至饰面板。直接粘贴法须确保基层平整度≤2mm/2m【依赖冲筋找平】。
- *工具*：钉枪、美工刀、卷尺。
- *验收*：防潮膜连续；搭接≥100mm；无破损。
**Step 3: 挂装龙骨安装（如适用）**
- *描述*：对于较大板材或基层不平整处，用不锈钢紧固件安装镀锌钢或铝合金龙骨系统（25×25mm或38×38mm）。龙骨系统可调平并预留管线空间。
- *工具*：激光水平仪、冲击起子、水平尺。
- *验收*：龙骨垂直且水平；固定间距≤600mm。
**Step 4: 板材裁切与边部封边**
- *描述*：用细齿面板锯将板材裁切至所需尺寸（防止木皮崩边）。**立即对所有裁切边进行封边处理**（蜡或透明清漆）——未封边的边缘是水分和白蚁入侵的主要通道。
- *工具*：面板锯、封边剂、毛刷。
- *验收*：切口干净；所有裁切边在30分钟内完成封边。
**Step 5: 板材固定（直接粘贴或挂装）**
- *描述*：
  - **直接粘贴**：在板背以蛇形打胶（聚氨酯基建筑胶），然后用力压贴于基层。用临时支撑固定板材直至胶粘剂固化。
  - **挂装**：用不锈钢螺丝或卡扣将板材固定于龙骨。所有板缝处**预留3–5mm伸缩缝**。
- *工具*：胶枪、螺丝枪、夹具/支撑、3–5mm定位垫片。
- *验收*：板材固定牢固；伸缩缝一致；板与板之间无接触。
**Step 6: 伸缩缝填充**
- *描述*：用**颜色匹配的柔性填缝料**（丙烯酸或硅酮基）填充伸缩缝，可适应±1mm的位移。禁止使用刚性填缝料（环氧/聚酯）——木材移动时会开裂。
- *工具*：填缝枪、压缝工具、湿海绵。
- *验收*：填缝饱满；填缝料柔韧且颜色匹配。
**Step 7: 表面涂装（如需要）**
- *描述*：根据饰面类型，涂布防护面漆（哑光/缎面清漆、OSMO木蜡油或UV固化涂层）。印尼高湿地区**建议最少2道面漆**以密封表面防潮。
- *工具*：喷枪或滚筒、细砂纸（层间用320目）。
- *验收*：涂层均匀；无流挂、无橘皮；表面呈水珠效果。
### 验收标准 (QC)
| 验收项 | 标准 | 检测方法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **板材含水率** | 安装时8–12% | 含水率测试仪 |
| **伸缩缝** | 所有接缝处3–5mm | 塞尺 |
| **基层平整度** | ≤ 2mm/2m | 2m靠尺 |
| **固定牢固度** | 手推无位移 | 手压测试 |
| **封边覆盖率** | 裁切边100%封边 | 目视 |
| **表面涂层** | 无流挂、橘皮 | 目视+光泽度仪 |
### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）
| 问题 | 原因 | 解决方案 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **板材翘曲/瓦变** | 吸湿不均匀 / 未做平衡 | 现场平衡7天；确保两面湿度均衡 |
| **伸缩缝闭合（挤压）** | 预留缝不足 / 高湿膨胀 | 强制预留3–5mm缝；使用柔性填缝料 |
| **白蚁蛀蚀** | 未处理板材 / 接触地面 | **强制**窑干+浸渍处理；保持离地50mm间距 |
| **边缘分层** | 未封边 / 水分侵入 | 立即封边所有裁切边；涂装2道面漆 |
| **紧固件锈蚀（吐锈）** | 沿海地区使用非不锈钢件 | 最低使用304级不锈钢 |

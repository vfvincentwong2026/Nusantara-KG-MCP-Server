# Wallpaper / Fabric Wallcovering Installation (壁纸/墙布铺贴工艺)
> 🤖 本文件为高精度饰面工法模板，带 ⚠️ 的参数依据《JGJ/T 29-2015 建筑涂饰工程施工及验收规程》及国际壁纸协会（IGI）安装标准，具体项目需依据选用的壁纸材料特性调整。

## English (EN)
### Overview
Wallpaper and fabric wallcoverings provide a premium aesthetic with texture, pattern, and warmth that paint cannot achieve. However, this system is **extremely sensitive to substrate conditions** — any imperfection in flatness, moisture, or cleanliness will telegraph through the finished surface. In Indonesia's tropical climate, **mold growth** and **adhesive failure due to high humidity** are the primary risks, demanding strict material selection and environmental controls.
### Critical Process Parameters
| Parameter | Specification | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Substrate Flatness (Pre-work)** | **≤ 1.5mm / 2m** ⚠️ | Any deviation >1.5mm will show through |
| **Substrate Moisture Content** | **< 8%** (mandatory test) ⚠️ | Critical for Indonesia's humid climate |
| **Ambient Temperature** | 15–30°C ⚠️ | — |
| **Relative Humidity (RH)** | **≤ 75%** during application ⚠️ | Use dehumidifier if exceeding |
| **Adhesive Open Time** | 5–10 minutes (at 30°C) ⚠️ | Indonesia's heat shortens working window |
| **Seam Overlap** | 1–2mm (for overlap seams) | Trimmed with straightedge and knife |
| **Drying Time Before Trimming** | 24–48 hours ⚠️ | Prevents shrinkage gaps |
### Step-by-Step Workflow
**Step 1: Substrate Inspection & Preparation**
- *Description*: Inspect the wall surface for cracks, pinholes, and unevenness. Sand down high spots and fill low spots with fine putty. Apply a **high-penetration sealing primer** to unify absorption and block residual moisture. **Test substrate moisture content** — if >8%, delay installation and use dehumidifiers.
- *Tools*: Sanding block (180–220 grit), putty knife, moisture meter, roller.
- *Acceptance*: Surface smooth, dry, and uniformly absorbent.
**Step 2: Wallcovering Layout & Cutting**
- *Description*: Measure wall height and add 100–150mm allowance for top/bottom trimming. Cut multiple strips simultaneously (batch cutting) to ensure pattern matching across seams. For patterned wallcoverings, **match the pattern repeat** precisely — misalignment is visually obvious and unacceptable.
- *Tools*: Measuring tape, straightedge, utility knife, cutting table.
- *Acceptance*: All strips cut to correct length; pattern match aligned within ±2mm.
**Step 3: Adhesive Mixing & Application**
- *Description*: Use a **premium-grade, anti-mold formulated adhesive** (starch-based for paper, acrylic-based for non-woven/fabric). Mix according to manufacturer's instructions. Apply adhesive evenly to the back of the wallcovering (for paper) or to the wall surface (for non-woven) using a roller or brush. **Do not over-apply** — excess adhesive causes bubbling and staining.
- *Tools*: Adhesive roller, brush, mixing bucket.
- *Acceptance*: Adhesive coverage uniform; no dry spots; no pooling.
**Step 4: Hanging & Positioning**
- *Description*: Hang the first strip using a plumb line or laser as a vertical reference. Position the strip with a 50mm overlap onto the ceiling and adjacent wall. Use a **smoothing brush or plastic smoother** to expel air bubbles from center outward. For non-woven, the adhesive is applied to the wall, so the dry strip is simply pressed into place.
- *Tools*: Smoothing brush, plastic smoother, seam roller.
- *Acceptance*: Strip垂直; no air bubbles; edges flush with reference line.
**Step 5: Seam Rolling & Joint Finishing**
- *Description*: For overlap seams, use a sharp utility knife with a straightedge to trim through both layers, removing the top overlap. Remove the trimmed strip and roll the seam with a **seam roller** to ensure full adhesion. For butt seams, press edges together firmly and roll.
- *Tools*: Utility knife, straightedge, seam roller.
- *Acceptance*: Seams invisible to the eye at 1m distance; no lifting edges.
**Step 6: Top & Bottom Trimming**
- *Description*: After the adhesive has set (20–30 minutes), use a sharp knife and straightedge to trim excess material at the ceiling line and baseboard line. **Replace the knife blade frequently** — a dull blade will tear rather than cut cleanly.
- *Tools*: Utility knife (fresh blade), straightedge, putty knife (as guide).
- *Acceptance*: Cuts clean and straight; no frayed edges.
**Step 7: Curing & Environmental Control**
- *Description*: Allow 24–48 hours for full drying. **Keep windows closed** during this period to prevent rapid drying (which causes seam separation) and to avoid dust contamination. In Indonesia's rainy season, run dehumidifiers to maintain RH ≤ 65%.
- *Tools*: Dehumidifier (if needed), fans (indirect only).
- *Acceptance*: Wallcovering fully adhered; no bubbles; seams tight.
### Quality Control (QC) Standards
| Criterion | Standard | Inspection Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Substrate Flatness** | ≤ 1.5mm gap under 2m straightedge | Feeler gauge |
| **Substrate Moisture** | < 8% | Electronic moisture meter |
| **Seam Visibility** | Invisible at 1m distance | Visual inspection |
| **Pattern Match** | ≤ 2mm deviation at seams | Visual + tape measure |
| **Air Bubbles** | Zero | Visual + hand sweep |
| **Edge Adhesion** | No lifting or curling | Fingertip test |
### Common Issues & Prevention (Indonesia-Specific)
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mold Growth (Black Spots)** | High humidity / Non-anti-mold adhesive | **Must** use anti-mold adhesive; maintain RH ≤ 65% during curing |
| **Seam Separation (Gapping)** | Rapid drying / Insufficient adhesive | Keep windows closed; use seam adhesive for edges |
| **Bubbles / Blisters** | Air trapped / Adhesive applied unevenly | Use smoothing brush from center outward; re-roll after 10 mins |
| **Pattern Misalignment** | Incorrect cutting / Wall not plumb | Use laser level; cut with pattern repeat marked |
| **Staining / Yellowing** | Substrate contamination / Adhesive bleed-through | Apply sealing primer; avoid over-applying adhesive |
### Related Knowledge (Labor, Materials & Alternatives)
- **Labor**: Requires **[[wallpaper-specialist|Wallcovering Specialist]]** — pattern matching and seam trimming demand significant skill.
- **Primary Adhesive**: [[anti-mold-wallpaper-adhesive|Anti-Mold Starch/Acrylic Adhesive]].
- **Substrate Prep**: [[high-penetration-primer|High-Penetration Sealing Primer]] + [[fine-finish-putty|Fine Wall Putty]].
- **Alternative Finish**: [[premium-wall-paint|Premium Wall Paint]] (less pattern, more durable) / [[wood-veneer-wall|Wood Veneer Panels]] (more premium, higher cost).

## 中文 (ZH)
### 概览
壁纸和织物墙布提供涂料无法比拟的质感、图案和温暖感。然而，该系统对**基层条件极度敏感**——任何平整度、含水率或清洁度方面的瑕疵都会在完成面上暴露无遗。在印尼热带气候下，**霉菌滋生**和**高湿导致的胶粘剂失效**是首要风险，要求严格的材料选择和环境保护措施。
### 核心工艺参数
| 参数 | 规格指标 | 备注 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **施工前基层平整度** | **≤ 1.5mm / 2m** ⚠️ | 偏差>1.5mm会透出 |
| **基层含水率** | **< 8%（强制检测）** ⚠️ | 印尼高湿气候关键指标 |
| **环境温度** | 15–30°C ⚠️ | — |
| **施工相对湿度（RH）** | **≤ 75%** ⚠️ | 超标须使用除湿机 |
| **胶粘剂开放时间** | 5–10分钟（30°C环境）⚠️ | 高温缩短可操作窗口 |
| **搭接重叠量** | 1–2mm（搭接法） | 用直尺和刀具裁切 |
| **裁切前干燥时间** | 24–48小时 ⚠️ | 防止收缩产生缝隙 |
### 工序流程
**Step 1: 基层检查与处理**
- *描述*：检查墙面裂纹、针孔和不平整处。打磨高点，用细腻子填补低点。滚涂**高渗透封闭底漆**以统一吸收率并阻断残留水分。**检测基层含水率**——若>8%，须推迟施工并使用除湿机。
- *工具*：打磨块（180–220目）、刮刀、含水率测试仪、滚筒。
- *验收*：表面光滑、干燥、吸收率均匀。
**Step 2: 壁纸排版与裁切**
- *描述*：测量墙高，上下各加100–150mm余量。多幅同时裁切（批量裁切）以确保拼接处花型对齐。**有花型的壁纸须精确对花**——错位目视明显且不可接受。
- *工具*：卷尺、直尺、美工刀、裁切台。
- *验收*：所有幅条长度正确；花型对齐偏差≤±2mm。
**Step 3: 胶粘剂调配与涂布**
- *描述*：使用**高品质防霉配方胶粘剂**（纸基用淀粉基，无纺布/织物用丙烯酸基）。按厂家说明调配。用滚筒或毛刷将胶粘剂均匀涂布于壁纸背面（纸基）或墙面（无纺布）。**不可过量涂布**——多余胶水会导致起泡和透胶污染。
- *工具*：胶粘剂滚筒、毛刷、搅拌桶。
- *验收*：胶粘剂涂布均匀；无干区；无堆积。
**Step 4: 上墙与定位**
- *描述*：用铅垂线或激光仪作为垂直基准，悬挂第一幅。将壁纸定位，顶部和侧边各预留50mm搭接量。用**压平刷或塑料刮板**从中心向四周赶出气泡。无纺布因胶水涂于墙面，只需将干壁纸压入即可。
- *工具*：压平刷、塑料刮板、接缝压轮。
- *验收*：壁纸垂直；无气泡；边缘与基准线齐平。
**Step 5: 接缝滚压与修整**
- *描述*：搭接缝处，用锋利美工刀配合直尺一次裁切两层，去除上层搭接部分。取出裁下的窄条，用**接缝压轮**滚压接缝以确保充分粘合。对碰缝则直接将边缘压紧后滚压。
- *工具*：美工刀、直尺、接缝压轮。
- *验收*：1m距离目视接缝不可见；边缘无翘起。
**Step 6: 上下端裁切**
- *描述*：胶粘剂初凝后（20–30分钟），用锋利刀具配合直尺裁切顶线和踢脚线处多余部分。**频繁更换刀片**——钝刀会撕裂而非切断。
- *工具*：美工刀（新刀片）、直尺、刮刀（作引导）。
- *验收*：切口干净平直；无毛边。
**Step 7: 养护与环境控制**
- *描述*：静置24–48小时充分干燥。期间**关闭门窗**以防过快干燥（导致接缝开裂）并避免灰尘污染。印尼雨季须开启除湿机，维持RH≤65%。
- *工具*：除湿机（按需）、风扇（仅间接通风）。
- *验收*：壁纸完全贴合；无气泡；接缝紧密。
### 验收标准 (QC)
| 验收项 | 标准 | 检测方法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **基层平整度** | 2m靠尺下缝隙≤1.5mm | 塞尺 |
| **基层含水率** | < 8% | 电子含水率测试仪 |
| **接缝可见度** | 1m距离不可见 | 目视 |
| **花型对齐** | 接缝处偏差≤2mm | 目视+卷尺 |
| **气泡** | 零气泡 | 目视+手扫 |
| **边缘粘合** | 无翘起、无卷边 | 指尖触感 |
### 常见问题与避坑（印尼特化）
| 问题 | 原因 | 解决方案 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **霉变（黑斑）** | 高湿环境 / 非防霉胶粘剂 | **必须**使用防霉胶粘剂；养护期间维持RH≤65% |
| **接缝开裂** | 干燥过快 / 胶量不足 | 关闭门窗；接缝处补涂专用接缝胶 |
| **气泡/鼓包** | 空气 trapped / 涂胶不均 | 从中心向外压平；10分钟后再次滚压 |
| **花型错位** | 裁切错误 / 墙面不垂直 | 使用激光水平仪；裁切时标注花型重复位置 |
| **透胶污染/黄变** | 基层污染 / 胶水渗出 | 涂刷封闭底漆；避免过量涂胶 |
### 关联知识（人力、材料与替代方案）
- **技术工人**：需[[wallpaper-specialist|壁纸专项技师]]——对花和接缝修整需要相当技能。
- **主胶粘剂**：[[anti-mold-wallpaper-adhesive|防霉淀粉基/丙烯酸基壁纸胶]]。
- **基层准备**：[[high-penetration-primer|高渗透封闭底漆]] + [[fine-finish-putty|细面墙泥]]。
- **替代饰面**：[[premium-wall-paint|高端乳胶漆]]（无花型、更耐久）/ [[wood-veneer-wall|木饰面挂板]]（更高级、成本更高）。

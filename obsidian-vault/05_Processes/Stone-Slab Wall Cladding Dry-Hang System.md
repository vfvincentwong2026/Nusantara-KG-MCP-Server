# Stone / Slab Wall Cladding Dry-Hang System（石材/岩板墙面干挂系统）

> 🤖 本文件为结构性墙面干挂工法，适用于地震带及高层建筑。严禁使用木龙骨或非不锈钢构件。

---

## English（EN）

### Overview

Dry-hang cladding uses mechanical anchors to secure stone or sintered stone slabs to a stainless steel sub-frame. Unlike wet-adhesive methods, this system accommodates building movement and seismic activity. The 30–50mm air cavity behind the cladding provides drainage and ventilation—critical for Indonesia's high humidity.

### Critical Process Parameters

|Parameter|Specification|Note|
|---|---|---|
|**Slab Thickness**|20–30mm (stone) / 6–12mm (sintered)|—|
|**Sub-frame Material**|Stainless steel (304 min.)|Anti-corrosion mandatory|
|**Anchor Spacing**|≤ 600mm vertical / ≤ 400mm horizontal|Seismic standard|
|**Expansion Joint**|5–8mm at all panel junctions|—|
|**Cavity Depth**|30–50mm|Drainage/ventilation|

### Step-by-Step Workflow

**Step 1: Sub-frame Installation**

- _Description_: Install stainless steel vertical and horizontal profiles on the wall using expansion anchors. Level and plumb the frame with laser levels. All fixings must be stainless steel—carbon steel will rust within 12 months.
    
- _Acceptance_: Frame plumb (±2mm/2m); anchors torqued to spec.
    

**Step 2: Anchor Slot Cutting**

- _Description_: Cut slots or drill holes on the slab back for mechanical anchors. Slot depth and position must match the sub-frame profile.
    
- _Acceptance_: Slot depth consistent; no cracks at slot edges.
    

**Step 3: Slab Mounting & Fixing**

- _Description_: Lift slabs with suction cups. Engage anchors into the sub-frame and secure with set-screws. Use 3–5mm spacer shims between slabs for expansion joints.
    
- _Acceptance_: Slabs secure; expansion joints uniform.
    

**Step 4: Joint Sealing**

- _Description_: Fill expansion joints with flexible silicone sealant (color-matched). Do not use rigid fillers—seismic movement will crack them.
    
- _Acceptance_: Joints sealed; silicone smooth and continuous.
    

### Quality Control（QC）

|Criterion|Standard|Inspection Method|
|---|---|---|
|**Frame Plumb**|≤2mm/2m|Laser level|
|**Anchor Pull-out**|≥1.5 kN|Pull test (random)|
|**Expansion Joint**|5–8mm uniform|Feeler gauge|
|**Slab Flatness**|≤2mm/2m|2m straightedge|

### Common Issues & Prevention（Indonesia-Specific）

|Issue|Cause|Solution|
|---|---|---|
|**Frame Rust**|Carbon steel fixings|**Mandatory** stainless steel 304|
|**Slab Cracking (Seismic)**|No expansion joints / Rigid anchors|5–8mm joints; flexible anchors|
|**Anchor Pull-out**|Insufficient embedment|≥60mm embedment into concrete|

## 中文（ZH）

### 概览

干挂系统通过机械锚固将石材或岩板固定于不锈钢骨架。与湿贴法不同，该系统可适应建筑位移和地震活动。30–50mm空腔提供排水和通风——对印尼高湿环境至关重要。

### 核心工艺参数

|参数|规格指标|备注|
|---|---|---|
|**板材厚度**|20–30mm（石材）/ 6–12mm（岩板）|—|
|**骨架材料**|不锈钢（304级）|防腐强制项|
|**锚固间距**|竖向≤600mm / 水平≤400mm|抗震标准|
|**伸缩缝**|板缝5–8mm|—|
|**空腔深度**|30–50mm|排水/通风|

### 工序流程

**Step 1: 骨架安装** → 膨胀锚栓固定不锈钢龙骨，激光校垂直/水平。  
**Step 2: 锚固开槽** → 板背开槽或钻孔，位置与龙骨匹配。  
**Step 3: 板材挂装** → 吸盘起吊，机械锚固入龙骨，用垫片控制缝宽。  
**Step 4: 填缝密封** → 伸缩缝填充柔性硅酮胶（颜色匹配）。

### 验收标准（QC）

|验收项|标准|检测方法|
|---|---|---|
|**骨架垂直度**|≤2mm/2m|激光水平仪|
|**锚固抗拔力**|≥1.5 kN|随机拉拔测试|
|**伸缩缝**|5–8mm均匀|塞尺|
|**板面平整度**|≤2mm/2m|2m靠尺|

# Wet Method Tiling (湿铺法地砖铺贴)

## English (EN)
### Overview
The Wet Method Tiling process is the most common flooring solution in the Indonesian archipelago. It involves laying tiles over a 30–50mm thick **cement-sand mortar** bed. Due to the humid tropical climate and the common use of large-format porcelain and marble tiles, this method demands meticulous execution at every step—from substrate preparation to curing.
### Process Parameters
- **Thickness**: 30–50mm ⚠️
- **Flatness Tolerance**: < 3mm per 2m straightedge ⚠️
- **Hollow Ratio Standard**: < 3% per tile (main corridors must have zero hollow spots) ⚠️
### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. **Substrate Preparation**  
   Remove dust, debris, and oil stains. Dampen the substrate thoroughly. In Indonesia's high-temperature environment, the substrate loses moisture rapidly — moisten at least 2 hours prior to installation.  
   - *Tools*: Broom, mop, water hose.  
   - *Acceptance*: No standing water, no dust, no loose sand.
2. **1:3 Cement-Sand Screed**  
   Mix cement and sand at a 1:3 ratio. Apply at a thickness of 30–50mm and level with a screed bar.  
   - *Tools*: Shovel, trowel, 2m straightedge, screed bar.  
   - *Acceptance*: Flatness tolerance ≤ 3mm per 2m ⚠️.
3. **Back-Buttering the Tile**  
   Apply a neat cement paste to the back of the tile. Use a notched trowel (10mm notch) to create uniform grooves.  
   - *Tools*: Notched trowel, trowel.  
   - *Acceptance*: Paste is full and even; no missing spots at corners.
4. **Placement & Bedding**  
   Lay the tile onto the mortar bed. Tap from the center outward with a rubber mallet to expel air and ensure full contact.  
   - *Tools*: Rubber mallet, spirit level, laser level.  
   - *Acceptance*: All corners flush; adjacent tile height difference ≤ 0.5mm ⚠️.
5. **Leveling System Installation**  
   Use tile leveling clips and pliers to lock the joint width and prevent movement during curing.  
   - *Tools*: Leveling clips, leveling pliers.  
   - *Acceptance*: Joint widths consistent; clips fully engaged — this is the **critical step** to prevent hollow spots.
6. **Curing**  
   No foot traffic for 24 hours after installation. During Indonesia's rainy season, ensure proper indoor ventilation and dehumidification.  
   - *Tools*: Warning tape.  
   - *Acceptance*: No footprints, no displacement.
7. **Grouting / Sealing**  
   After 24–48 hours, clean the joints, apply epoxy sealant, and shape the finish.  
   - *Tools*: Sealant gun, shaping ball, scraper.  
   - *Acceptance*: Joint width uniform (1.5–2.5mm) ⚠️; sealant color matched to tile.
### Quality Control (QC) Standards
| Criterion | Standard | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hollow Ratio | Corner hollow < 3% per tile; **zero hollow** on main walkways ⚠️ | Tapping sound test |
| Flatness | Gap ≤ 1mm under 2m straightedge ⚠️ | — |
| Slope (Bathroom) | Slope towards floor drain; water flows freely without pooling | Water pouring test |
| Joint Width | Uniform 1.5–2.5mm ⚠️ | Visual + caliper |
| Color | Sealant color matches or harmonizes with tile | Visual |
### Common Issues & Prevention
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hollow Spots | Substrate not dampened / Insufficient paste | Dampen before laying; apply full paste coverage |
| Uneven Surface | Leveling system not used | Mandatory use of leveling clips |
| Lippage | Tile warpage / Improper bedding | Select flat tiles; tap firmly with rubber mallet |
| Efflorescence | Cement alkali leaching; worsened by high humidity | Use anti-efflorescence backing coating; switch to thin-set method |
| Dampness (Rainy Season) | Substrate moisture too high | Test substrate moisture before work; delay if necessary |

## 中文 (ZH)
### 概览
湿铺法地砖工艺是努山塔拉群岛最常用的地面铺装方案。该工艺将瓷砖铺设在 30–50mm 厚的**水泥砂浆**垫层上。鉴于当地湿热的热带气候以及大规格瓷质砖和石材的普遍使用，此工法要求从基层处理到养护的每一步都必须严谨执行。
### 工艺参数
- **铺贴厚度**：30–50mm ⚠️
- **平整度公差**：2m 靠尺检查，误差 < 3mm ⚠️
- **空鼓率标准**：单块砖边角空鼓 < 3%，主要通道严禁空鼓 ⚠️
### 工序流程
1. **基层处理**  
   清理浮灰、油污，洒水湿润基层。印尼高温环境下基层失水快，需提前 2 小时湿润。  
   - *工具*：扫把、拖把、水管。  
   - *验收*：基层无明水、无浮灰、无起砂。
2. **1:3 水泥砂浆找平**  
   水泥砂浆按 1:3 配比搅拌，铺设厚度 30–50mm，用刮杠刮平。  
   - *工具*：铁锹、抹子、2m 靠尺、刮杠。  
   - *验收*：平整度误差 < 3mm/2m ⚠️。
3. **瓷砖背面刮浆**  
   瓷砖背面涂抹纯水泥膏，使用齿形刮板（10mm 齿距）拉槽，槽深均匀。  
   - *工具*：齿形刮板、抹子。  
   - *验收*：刮浆饱满，边角无遗漏。
4. **铺贴与振实**  
   将瓷砖铺设在砂浆层上，用橡皮锤由中心向四周振实排气。  
   - *工具*：橡皮锤、水平尺、激光水平仪。  
   - *验收*：四角平整，相邻砖高差 < 0.5mm ⚠️。
5. **调平器固定**  
   使用调平器系统固定缝隙，防止凝固过程中移位。  
   - *工具*：调平器、调平钳。  
   - *验收*：缝宽一致，调平器卡扣到位——**这是防止空鼓的关键工序**。
6. **养护**  
   铺贴完成 24 小时内禁止上人，印尼雨季需注意室内通风除湿。  
   - *工具*：警示带。  
   - *验收*：无踩踏痕迹、无移位。
7. **勾缝/美缝**  
   24–48 小时后清理缝隙，填充美缝剂，压缝成型。  
   - *工具*：美缝枪、压缝球、铲刀。  
   - *验收*：缝宽 1.5–2.5mm 均匀 ⚠️，美缝颜色与砖色协调。

### 常见问题与避坑
| 问题 | 原因 | 解决方案 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 空鼓 | 基层未湿润 / 砂浆不饱满 | 铺贴前洒水湿润，刮浆饱满 |
| 不平整 | 未使用调平器 | 强制使用调平器系统 |
| 接缝高低差 | 砖变形 / 铺贴不实 | 选砖挑平整度好的，橡皮锤振实 |
| 泛碱（白华） | 水泥碱分渗出，印尼高湿环境加剧 | 使用防碱背涂剂，或改瓷砖胶薄贴法 |
| 雨季返潮 | 基层含水率过高 | 雨季施工前测基层含水率，必要时延后 |

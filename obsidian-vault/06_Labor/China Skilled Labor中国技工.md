## English (EN)

### Overview
China Skilled Labor refers to senior craftsmen (typically from China) working on Indonesian premium interior projects. They are the **quality backbone** of high-end delivery: their craftsmanship on large-format slabs, microcement, and complex curved work is effectively irreplaceable by local crews. Although their daily rate is 1.5–2.5× that of local skilled workers, their near-zero rework rate usually makes them the **lowest total cost** option for craftsmanship-critical processes.

### Suitable Processes
- [[large-format-slab-installation|Large-Format Slab / Sintered Stone Installation]] — mandatory for slabs ≥1.2m
- [[microcement-wall-finishing|Microcement Wall Finishing]] — four-pass troweling requires trained hands
- [[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips|Ribbed Screed / Gauging Strips Leveling]] — extreme verticality tolerance
- [[floating-suspended-ceiling|Floating / Suspended Ceiling]] — complex light-gauge steel structures
- [[custom-wood-millwork-curved|Custom Wood Millwork / Curved Wall System]] — curved and irregular forms
- [[stone-seamless-crystallization|Stone Seamless Joint & Crystallization]]
- [[stone-slab-wall-cladding|Stone-Slab Wall Cladding Dry-Hang System]]
- [[wood-veneer-wall|Wood Veneer Panel Wall Installation]]
- [[premium-wall-paint-art-finish|Premium Wall Paint / Art Paint Finish]]
- [[wet-method-tiling|Wet Method Tiling]] — only when large-format slabs are involved

### Cost & Efficiency Reference
- **Daily rate**: Rp 450,000–650,000 ⚠️
- **Efficiency**: ~1.5 man-days per 10m² (large-format tiling) ⚠️
- **Cost logic**: slow but precise; rework rate near zero, so total cost is optimal on premium work ⚠️

### Suitable Scenarios ✅
- Minimalist styles (seamless, no baseboard, narrow trims)
- Oversized materials (1.2m × 2.4m sintered stone feature walls)
- Complex geometry (curved walls, irregular ceilings)
- Owners with extremely high fidelity expectations

### Not Suitable ❌
- Standard-format tiles (≤800×800mm) — local skilled labor is more cost-effective
- Simple skim coat / basic leveling — general labor suffices
- Extremely tight schedules — senior crews are typically booked in advance ⚠️

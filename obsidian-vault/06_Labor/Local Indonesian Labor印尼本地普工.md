## English (EN)

### Overview
Local Indonesian Labor (kuli / pekerja umum) are general workers for **auxiliary and low-skill tasks**. They are the most abundant and cheapest workforce tier in Indonesia, available on short notice in any city. Their role in premium projects is strictly bounded: logistics, demolition, cleaning, backfill assistance, and protection. **Any finish-critical process executed independently by general labor is a proven failure pattern** in Indonesian high-end delivery.

### Suitable Tasks (Auxiliary Only)
- [[finished-works-protection-system|Finished Works Protection]] — laying protection boards/film, daily checks
- Site cleaning and debris removal (before/after each trade)
- Material handling and on-site logistics
- Demolition and strip-out works
- [[bathroom-waterproofing-cistern-system|Cistern backfill assistance]] — LECA transport and placement **under skilled supervision only**
- [[wet-method-tiling|Wet method tiling assistance]] — mixing mortar, carrying tiles (no laying)
- [[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips|Screed assistance]] — material supply only

### Cost & Efficiency Reference
- **Daily rate**: Rp 120,000–180,000 ⚠️
- **Efficiency**: ~0.8 man-day per 10m² (auxiliary tasks) ⚠️
- **Cost logic**: cheapest per head, but unsupervised output is unreliable — supervision cost must be budgeted ⚠️

### Suitable Scenarios ✅
- Finished-works protection installation and maintenance
- Cleaning, demolition, material logistics
- Backfill and mortar-mixing assistance under direct command
- Peak-period manpower supplement

### Not Suitable ❌
- **Any** finish process (tiling, painting, microcement, joinery) — must not execute independently
- Waterproofing layer application — assist only, never apply
- Tasks requiring measurement or tolerance judgment
- Working unsupervised on site ⚠️

## 中文 (ZH)

### 概览
印尼本地普工（kuli / pekerja umum）是从事**辅助与低技能工序**的通用劳动力，印尼供给最充足、成本最低的人力层级，任何城市都可快速召集。在高端项目中角色必须严格限定：搬运、拆除、清理、回填辅助、成品保护。**普工独立承担任何面层关键工序，是印尼高端交付中已被反复验证的翻车模式**。

### 适合工序（仅限辅助）
- [[finished-works-protection-system|成品保护]]——铺设保护板/保护膜、每日检查
- 现场清理与建渣清运（各工种前后）
- 材料搬运与现场物流
- 拆除与旧装剥离
- [[bathroom-waterproofing-cistern-system|沉箱回填辅助]]——陶粒运输与摊铺，**必须在技工监督下进行**
- [[wet-method-tiling|湿铺法辅助]]——拌砂浆、搬砖（不得上手铺贴）
- [[ribbed-screed-gauging-strips|找平辅助]]——仅供料

### 工时与成本参考
- **日薪**：Rp 120,000–180,000 ⚠️
- **效率**：辅助工序约 0.8 工日/10m² ⚠️
- **成本逻辑**：人头最便宜，但无监督产出不可靠——必须把监督成本计入预算 ⚠️

### 适用场景 ✅
- 成品保护的安装与维护
- 清理、拆除、材料物流
- 直接指挥下的回填与拌浆辅助
- 高峰期人力补充

### 不适用场景 ❌
- **任何**面层工序（贴砖、油漆、微水泥、木作）——严禁独立施工
- 防水层涂刷——只能辅助，严禁上手
- 需要测量或容差判断的工序
- 无监督在场作业 ⚠️
